## Proposed bridge and residual

Two preparer proposals would add 30 and subtract 12 from the ledger gross grouping: 1,200 + 30 - 12 = **1,218**. Against the target of 1,210, residual (proposed result minus target) is **+8**. Retain that residual for investigation. Do not create an unsupported -8 entry and call the work complete. The accepted bridge is still unknown because neither proposal has supporting authority or approval.

If an actual reviewer later rejects the +30 proposal while the -12 remains only proposed, the remaining candidate is 1,188 and the residual is -22. Preserve this as a new scenario, not a replacement for the original ledger or a claim that -12 is accepted. The sign convention must be visible in every scenario.

Net amounts reveal another check: the initial ledger is 1,200 - 300 = 900, while the proposed gross bridge implies 1,218 - 300 = 918. The target net 910 differs by 8 under the assumed unchanged contra balance. If contra balances also need adjustment, this shortcut is invalid; analyze them separately.

## Counterexample: identical net balance, different gross presentation

A second fictional source shows gross 1,240 and contra 340, yielding net 900. The first ledger also yields net 900, but each gross/contra figure differs by 40. Matching net totals does not resolve source completeness, account classification or presentation. Obtain underlying detail and applicable instructions rather than accepting the tie.

## Counterexample: entity differences cancel

Entity differences are +20 and -20: net zero, gross absolute differences **40**. Investigate both; do not infer that an intercompany reclassification is permitted or that either target is correct.
