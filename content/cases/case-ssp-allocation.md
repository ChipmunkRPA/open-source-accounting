## Confirmed hypothetical assumptions
Two distinct obligations have already been established by a qualified reviewer. The fixed transaction price is 90,000; confirmed standalone selling prices are 80,000 for a subscription and 20,000 for training. No allocation exception, variable consideration, tax, significant financing effect, or other adjustment is assumed. This case does not establish those accounting conclusions. ## Calculation
Total standalone prices = 80,000 + 20,000 = 100,000. Subscription allocation = 90,000 × 80,000 / 100,000 = 72,000. Training allocation = 90,000 × 20,000 / 100,000 = 18,000. The allocations sum to the fixed price of 90,000.

## Timing remains separate
Even after allocation, the facts do not say when revenue should be recognized. Obtain evidence about the transfer of each promised item and verify the appropriate current guidance. The calculation must not automatically recognize training on invoice date or allocate cash receipts as if they were revenue.

## Inputs that change the case
