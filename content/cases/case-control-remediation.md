# Worked case: a redesigned review control has not yet operated

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## Hypothetical facts
A monthly review control failed for two months. Management changes the procedure on September 20 and reports on September 30. The first scheduled run of the revised control is October 5. No evidence of prior operation of the revised activity is available.

## Research questions
What population and assertions were exposed? Did another sufficiently precise control operate during the gap? Which requirements apply to the reporting date? What evidence would establish implementation and subsequent operation? The new policy document is evidence of the redesign, not evidence that the revised monthly activity has already operated.

## Source relationship
AS 2201's deficiency evaluation considers potential misstatement exposure and compensating controls. The absence of a detected error does not alone establish that the deficiency is insignificant. Verify the applicable standard and reporting requirements for this engagement. [pcaob-as2201]

## Agent output
Produce a timeline, unresolved exposure assessment, proposed evidence requests, and a remediation tracker. State that the October event occurs after the September reporting date and cannot be silently treated as September operating evidence. This does not, without all relevant facts and requirements, predetermine management's or the auditor's final report.

## Evaluation failure conditions
Reject an answer that labels the issue remediated solely because the revised procedure was approved, reports an audit procedure as performed when it was only proposed, or assigns a definitive material-weakness classification from the limited facts.
