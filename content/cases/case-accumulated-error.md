# Worked case: a liability error accumulates over three years

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## Hypothetical facts
An unsupported accrual increased by 12,000 in each of three years. None has reversed. Assume this is an error rather than a change in estimate, and that the additions reduced expense-adjusted profit in each originating year. No materiality conclusion has been made.

## Arithmetic
At the start of year three, the accumulated liability error is 24,000. The year-three addition is 12,000. The ending liability overstatement is 36,000. A full release through current-year expense would contain 24,000 attributable to earlier years as well as the 12,000 addition.

## Research task
SAB 108 is the entry point for considering both current-year effects and accumulated balance errors. SAB 99 supplies the separate qualitative-materiality perspective. Assess all affected financial statements and periods rather than concluding that a small annual addition makes the ending balance acceptable. [sec-sab108; sec-sab99]

## Missing information
Obtain pretax results and other relevant benchmarks, disclosures, prior-period facts, related errors, contractual thresholds, and management's rationale. Determine the accounting and legal implications through an appropriately reviewed process. The case does not decide whether filings must be amended or which correction presentation is required.

## Expected agent result
Return a signed error bridge, the two measurement views, separate materiality questions, and correction scenarios with unresolved assumptions. The result should not make a materiality decision from the numbers in this case alone.
