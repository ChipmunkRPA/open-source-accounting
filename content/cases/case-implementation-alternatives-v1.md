# Worked case: identical invoices can leave different implementation questions unanswered

**AI-assisted educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · AI editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## Shared fictional billing facts

A supplier bills USD 60000 for stated annual hosted access and USD 6000 for implementation. The combined invoice is USD 66000. The original packet lacks a useful statement of work, delivery evidence and complete acceptance terms.

The amounts and labels establish only the stipulated billing facts. They do not establish the accounting unit, allocation inputs or recognition timing. Primary ASC text was not accessed. [fasb-asc]

## Branch A: a described reusable deliverable

Operations supplies a description of a migration package that the customer can retain and use in another environment. Request the actual deliverable, evidence of usability, dependencies on the supplier, acceptance records and the governing agreement.

The description supports a different research path from internal activation, but retaining a file does not by itself settle the accounting criteria. A generated summary is not evidence that the package works or that the customer obtained particular legal rights.

## Branch B: described internal activation

Operations instead describes supplier account setup with no separate customer-facing output mentioned. Ask whether the description is complete, what was promised, whether other deliverables exist and how the fee relates to the overall arrangement. Do not infer that no transfer occurred solely because it was omitted from a brief interview note.

Nor does a conclusion about one activity by itself establish the recognition period for all fees. Remaining promises, renewal terms and applicable requirements need their own analysis.

## A comparison workpaper

| Question | Branch A | Branch B | What remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stated activity | Reusable migration package | Internal activation | Complete operational and contractual description |
| Stated customer output | Retained file/package | No separate output mentioned | Whether the description is supported and complete |
| Billing total | 66000 | 66000 | Whether billing equals an accounting price or unit |
| Next evidence | Delivered output and usability/rights | Full activity list and actual promises | Current applicable criteria and alternatives |

For an original sensitivity, assume an analyst proposes USD 6000 entirely at a purported implementation completion date. A second analyst spreads that same fee over twelve assumed months, USD 500 per month. The totals match, but the timing differs. Neither proposed schedule is selected or approved by this exercise; equal totals cannot resolve the missing recognition evidence.

## Answer quality and review boundary

A useful response names the missing facts, preserves the two branches and identifies authorized primary research needed. It should not fabricate a quotation, declare implementation always separate/deferred, or present the arithmetic as a final accounting answer.

The scenario supports a learning discussion and a factual evidence request. No real performance obligation, legal right, journal entry or professional conclusion is established.
