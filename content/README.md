The current library contains **124 original educational reader editions**. The articles remain educational drafts and are **not professionally reviewed**. They are not authoritative accounting advice, complete standards or automatically approved Agent evidence.

Read or download the current editions through the links below. Earlier canonical article versions and their review records are retained for traceability; they are outside this active reading index. Reader wording changes are limited to labels and notices, with source and reader hashes bound in the [reader-edition index](reader-editions/index.json). Earlier check statements refer to the original article revision; no new professional review or current-authority verification is claimed.

**Ray Sang Annotation** identifies our original explanations, interpretations and examples. It does not label underlying publisher/government text or imply Ray Sang's personal professional review. Original creator names, notices and license grants remain in force.
