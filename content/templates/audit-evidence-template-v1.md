## Independence inquiry and competence evidence

Use separate rows for a person's assertion, the underlying circumstances, applicable criteria and the decision. Record relevant relationships/services/interests as facts or unresolved questions; do not infer the absence of a relationship because no disclosure form was supplied. A signed assertion can establish that a statement was made under the stated conditions, while its accuracy, completeness and applicability still need evaluation.

For competence, identify the task-specific skills claimed, relevant experience, training record and assignment scope. Keep attendance assertions, course relevance, actual completion, supervision and permitted credit rules separate. A role title, subscription, user permission or model capability is not evidence of professional competence. This template does not determine which training qualifies or whether a person may accept an engagement.

## Original worked packet: attendance assertions are not competence

The fictional Rowan Audit Team is organizing one person's training records for one defined period. The exercise assumes that identical attendance-event IDs refer to the same event, with the same person/period and duration; no event crosses the cutoff. This is a duplicate-record teaching convention, not a regulatory credit rule. All durations are decimal hours supplied solely for arithmetic.

| Row | Person | Attendance event | Hours | Input status | Evidence state |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| T-01 | Rowan | EVT-A | 3 | confirmed | attendance asserted, not independently corroborated |
| T-01-copy | Rowan | EVT-A | 3 | confirmed | second export of the same asserted attendance event |
| T-02 | Rowan | EVT-B | 2 | confirmed | attendance asserted; course relevance unresolved |
| T-03 | Rowan | EVT-C | 4 | unknown | completion not known |
| T-04 | Rowan | EVT-D | 1 | assumption | planned future attendance only |

The unfiltered row sum is **3 + 3 + 2 + 4 + 1 = 13 hours**. It mixes duplicate, unknown and planned inputs and is not a valid completed-training total. Under the stated identity assumptions, the unique confirmed-assertion subtotal is **3 + 2 = 5 hours**. The five hours are still asserted attendance, not independently verified completion, allowed professional credit or demonstrated competence. Course eligibility, relevance, period rules and actual supporting records are unresolved.

A descriptive reconciliation is **13 = 5 unique asserted hours + 3 duplicate-export hours + 4 unresolved hours + 1 planned hour**. This identity checks the fictional arithmetic only. It does not resolve the underlying assertions or establish compliance with a threshold. The four unknown hours stay unknown and the planned hour stays an assumption; neither is silently promoted because the table balances.

## Counterexamples requiring different evidence

1. **Conflicting duplicates:** another export labels EVT-A as four hours rather than three. The identity/duration assumptions conflict. Preserve both records and abstain from a settled unique subtotal until the event, duration and revisions are reconciled; do not choose the convenient value or average them.
2. **Different people or periods:** the same event code appears for two people or across reporting periods. Event code alone no longer defines a duplicate. Use explicit person/event/period identities and investigate overlap; do not carry the five-hour result into a different population.
3. **Signed independence form, missing facts:** a preparer supplies a signed assertion but relationship/service history is incomplete. Record the assertion and missing evidence. No inference of independence or resolved threats follows from the signature alone.
4. **Planned procedure marked done:** the team writes “inspect supporting invoice” but has no actual executor/date, inspected revision or documented result. Keep the procedure proposed. A later genuine work record creates a new observed state; do not backdate performance to the original plan.
5. **Review of an old packet:** an authorized reviewer examined packet R-1, after which evidence or the draft changes in R-2. Preserve the original decision and exact scope. Do not claim that R-2 was reviewed, or that a self-review is independent, without an actual new decision covering those changes.

## Four study questions with draft expectations

1. **What does the five-hour subtotal establish?** Only a sum of unique supplied assertions under explicit fictional identity assumptions. It does not establish completed or eligible professional training.
2. **Can the unknown four hours become confirmed because a supervisor wants a larger total?** No new evidence has resolved completion. Preserve the unknown input and request the missing record.
3. **Does a signed form alone settle independence?** It records an assertion, while underlying circumstances, applicable criteria and actual decisions still need evaluation.
4. **When may the tracker say a procedure was performed and reviewed?** Record actual execution evidence and a real revision-specific review separately. A proposed method or uploaded document cannot supply either event.

These expectations are original unreviewed educational material, not human-adjudicated benchmarks. The four questions are inside this revised template and do not increase the separate question-bank count.
