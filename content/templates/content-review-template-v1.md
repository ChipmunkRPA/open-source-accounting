# Review a contributed article without mixing editorial, rights and professional approval

**AI-assisted educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · AI editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## Submission identity

Record the article ID, exact file/version/hash, author, original contribution, assistance used and sources cited. Inventory every borrowed passage, image, table and example. A public URL describes access, not automatically all permissions for storage, adaptation, model input or redistribution.

Preserve the actual submitted revision before editing. Describe changes and hash the final revision. A review receipt should say what was checked, against which sources, with what limitations; it should not manufacture a human reviewer or imply that an AI model is a licensed accountant.

## Four decisions, recorded separately

| Decision | Evidence to record | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial accuracy | Exact claims, sources actually examined, original example checks and unresolved gaps | Human professional approval or complete current-law verification |
| Human technical review | Actual reviewer identity, exact revision, scope, date and findings | Publisher permission or automatic independence/qualification verification |
| Rights review | Rights holder, operation, material/version, permission basis and conditions | Factual correctness, period applicability or payment entitlement |
| Product admission | Authorized operational decision for the exact permitted material and use | Broader access, endorsement or a license for unrelated sources |

These are proposed record fields. Blank fields remain blank. A contribution cannot authorize itself merely by writing “approved” in a table.

## Rights questions before copying or adapting

Identify which material is original, government-authored, openly licensed, separately licensed or unresolved. Check the rights for the actual operation and destination, including hosted/commercial use, storage, model processing, display, quotation, export and training. Separate source acquisition restrictions from downstream reuse conditions. Do not move confidential legal advice into a public article or receipt.

The U.S. Copyright Office describes fair use as case-specific and rejects a fixed word-count or percentage formula. Rewording alone is not a reliable rights clearance: assess the source material, purpose, amount, market effect and other applicable facts through the proper review process. This template makes no fair-use determination for a particular use. [fair-use]

CC BY 4.0 licenses rights the licensor can grant. Its legal code addresses attribution and identification of modifications, bars certain additional downstream restrictions and does not grant trademark rights. It does not relicense a separately cited private standard. The deed is a summary; the legal code should be examined for the actual terms. [cc-by-4]

## Claim-level technical review

For each material claim, record the exact article location, whether it is a factual statement, rule summary, inference or fictional assumption, and the primary source actually examined. Check dates, entity scope, exceptions, units, calculations and contrary evidence. Use “reference only” when the relevant passage was not accessed.

Original example: an article links a standard's homepage, then says that paragraph 40 requires a particular result. If paragraph 40 was not examined, the review must not report that requirement as verified. Either obtain authorized access and check the claim, or remove/reframe it as an unresolved research question. A working hyperlink is not a completed substantive review.

## Release record

List the publication decision, precise draft notice, remaining source gaps, attribution and next review triggers. Keep the educational article's license and access separate from paid Agent or private-workspace services. An article may remain a clearly limited educational draft while rights or technical work for a particular downstream use remains unresolved.

Record a new version for changed text. Reassess any prior approval affected by changed claims, sources, permissions or applicability. Do not set a database source's permissions or professional-review state from this checklist or an AI editorial receipt alone.
