## Operational checks
Review third-party embedded material, attribution, modification conditions, rate limits, permitted user groups, provider/region, retention, and takedown/deletion behavior. A user subscription is not a source license. Fail closed for unresolved operations.

## Version controls
Store canonical identity, issued/effective dates, hash, acquisition timestamp, and approval record. A revised source does not inherit all approvals automatically.
