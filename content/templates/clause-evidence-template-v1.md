# Contract clause-to-criterion matrix: trace the fact, interpretation and missing rule separately

**AI-assisted educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · AI editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## Purpose and document identity

Record the actual agreement, amendments, version/hash where available, parties, relevant period and the reason for review. List missing schedules, unreadable areas and extraction limitations. Do not invent a page number or treat an upload date as an operative effective date.

This original format connects evidence to research questions. Current primary ASC text was not accessed; candidate Topic or paragraph references remain unverified until the authorized passage and context have actually been examined. [fasb-asc]

## Matrix

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Evidence locator | Actual document/version/page or section; say unavailable if absent |
| Text or observation | Permitted short passage or accurate factual description |
| Evidence status | Confirmed, asserted, disputed, assumed or not inspected |
| Commercial/legal question | Meaning or effect requiring appropriate review |
| Accounting question | The decision that could change if the fact changes |
| Governing criterion | Exact authorized source/version/locator, or research-only reference |
| Supporting and contrary material | Both sides, with document identities |
| Missing prerequisite | Specific fact, source or approval still needed |
| Reviewer action | Actual decision, scope/date and exact revision if performed |

The fields are not a prescribed professional checklist, an automatic legal interpretation or a source-rights grant.

## Original filled example

Invented record C-01 says a fee is payable when invoiced. Invented record C-02 says the customer may reject incomplete delivery. Their actual legal effect is deliberately unresolved.

C-01 can support a question about billing terms. It does not by itself prove revenue timing. C-02 raises questions about delivery, acceptance and rights; it does not automatically establish a particular recognition date. A researcher should preserve both observations rather than choose whichever favors the desired outcome.

Suppose C-01 lists 12 monthly invoices of USD 700. Their arithmetic sum is USD 8400. The schedule still does not establish whether all months were billed, earned, collected or included in the relevant reporting period. Keep separate columns for each of those concepts if the facts require them.

## Conflicts and extraction limits

Where an amendment, schedule and main agreement conflict, record the issue and supporting evidence. Do not decide priority from file size, filename, matching keywords or a text-similarity score. Ask which operative terms apply and whether qualified legal interpretation is needed.

A page can contain relevant graphics, handwritten changes or text not represented in an extract. A missing retrieved passage is not proof of an absent clause. Verify every decisive claim against the source context needed for the conclusion.

## Review and release

Document what was actually inspected and why it supports the analysis. Keep proposed work in future tense. A completed matrix can be a useful draft while scope or authority remains unresolved; it must not be presented as professionally approved.

Store restricted passages only through the permitted process and audience. Public contributions should contain original explanation and lawful references rather than client secrets or copied standards.
