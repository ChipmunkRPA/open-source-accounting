Use distinct issue types: missing required disclosure, unclear wording, inconsistent facts, missing applicability decision, unsupported source claim and unreviewed area. Do not call something a required omission until the governing requirement and applicable facts have been verified.

## Original consistency example

An invented note lists three balances of USD 120, USD 180 and USD 200 and states a total of USD 570. The listed amounts sum to USD 500, a USD 70 difference. A separate management slide also reports USD 570 but does not identify its population.

The evidence supports a discrepancy, not a decision that USD 500 is the correct reportable amount. A missing USD 70 item, a mistyped total, different populations or different dates are competing explanations. Record the note and slide locations, preserve both versions and request the underlying reconciliation. Do not silently alter a disclosure to make the arithmetic tie.

## Source hierarchy and coverage

The SEC Financial Reporting Manual says it is non-authoritative and that underlying authoritative material governs a conflict. Its landing page inspected October 4, 2026 also warns that information can become outdated and distinguishes administrative revisions from technical section updates. Use it as a research aid, not as the authority for an alleged missing requirement. [sec-frm]

Peer disclosures can help identify questions or presentation alternatives. They do not prove that wording is required, complete or appropriate for this entity. Likewise, a matching keyword does not establish that the actual disclosure satisfies a requirement.

## Proposed edit and review history

For each candidate edit, show the old text, proposed text, factual basis, governing source if established and any changed implication. Identify commitments, legal positions or estimates introduced by the edit; those require the appropriate actual decision-makers. Assign names only when the people have really accepted the work.

Record who checked each issue, what they inspected and the exact revision. A row marked resolved should identify its supporting evidence and any remaining limitation. If the document changes, reassess affected issues rather than carrying a green status forward automatically.

## Completion boundary

Deliver the issue log, supporting calculations, source map, excluded areas and unresolved questions. The log is not a compliance certificate or permission to publish, submit a filing or contact a regulator. The public template remains freely readable; using it does not enable the separately gated disclosure-review Agent workflow.
