## Procedure tracker
Distinguish planned procedure, actual execution, result, exception follow-up, and reviewer sign-off. State which evidence supports the conclusion and what remains incomplete. Do not claim sufficient appropriate evidence solely because documents were uploaded.

## Data limitations
Record completeness/accuracy work, transformations, manual edits, missing pages, and all significant filters. Keep sensitive audit evidence in authorized private systems.
