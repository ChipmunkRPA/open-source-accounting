Editorial research outline · 2026-10-04 · No publisher standards text included

ESG is a collection of reporting and measurement systems, not a single interchangeable standard. Identify the intended users, the jurisdiction and legal basis, the reporting entity boundary, the reporting period, the materiality approach and the nature of the metric. Keep requirements, voluntary frameworks, calculation methodologies and assurance separate. These original research prompts do not reproduce the named standards or establish that a particular company must report.

A useful evidence packet includes an entity/jurisdiction map, legal-status ledger, metric dictionary, calculation inputs, unit conventions, estimation methods and data-owner sign-offs. Track the difference between a legal obligation and a chosen reporting framework. Financially focused reporting and impact-focused reporting may ask different questions. An emissions number also needs an organizational boundary, activity data, factors, units and a reproducible calculation; merely naming a framework does not establish measurement quality.

## Research tracks

### ISSB IFRS S1 and IFRS S2

Investor-focused sustainability-related disclosure research.

- Which jurisdiction has adopted which edition or modified requirements?
- What reporting boundary, materiality lens and time horizon apply?
- Which transition reliefs and local filing rules require confirmation?

Work product: A jurisdiction-and-period applicability matrix; no claim of universal legal adoption.

### SASB industry-based materials

Industry-specific sustainability research.

- Which business activities and industry classification describe the entity?
- Which metrics, boundaries and estimation methods need evidence?
- How does this use relate to an applicable reporting framework?

Work product: An original metric inventory with units, boundaries, owners and source-rights status.

### GRI Universal, Sector and Topic Standards

Impact-focused sustainability research.

- What impacts, stakeholders and material topics are relevant?
- Which applicable editions and sector guidance need review?
- How will metric evidence, omissions and reporting boundaries be documented?

Work product: An impact-topic research plan; publisher text requires an appropriate software/content permission.

### GHG Protocol standards and guidance

Greenhouse-gas inventory methodology research.

- What organizational and operational boundaries are chosen?
- What activity data, factors, units and calculation methods support each metric?
- What inventory changes, estimation limitations and assurance evidence must be tracked?

Work product: An original calculation-and-provenance workbook; standards text and tools retain their own terms.

### EU CSRD and ESRS legal texts

European legal requirements and amendments.

- Which undertaking, jurisdiction, reporting year and reporting wave apply?
- Which final Official Journal act and amendments govern the period?
- Is a document final law, consolidated aid, proposal, draft standard or implementation guidance?

Work product: A CELEX-based legal version chain and article/annex evidence map.

### U.S. federal and state sustainability disclosure requirements

Separate federal rules, state statutes, regulations and litigation status.

- What regulator and entity thresholds apply?
- Is the requirement proposed, adopted, stayed, amended or rescinded?
- What court orders or state implementation materials change the conclusion?

Work product: A jurisdiction-specific status ledger; do not transfer one regulator’s status to another.

## Evidence and review gates

Keep a direct quotation, an original paraphrase, a calculation and an assumption distinguishable. Record what source text was actually available and any unresolved access or rights gap.
