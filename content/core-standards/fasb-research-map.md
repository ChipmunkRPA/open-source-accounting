Editorial research outline · 2026-10-04 · No publisher standards text included

Start with a specific nongovernmental financial-reporting question. Record the entity, relevant transaction, contract dates and reporting period before choosing a research source. The outlines here are original questions and work-product designs; they do not restate or replace the Codification.

A useful first issue file contains the contract or transaction facts, proposed accounting alternatives, relevant source identifiers, effective-period evidence and the decisions that remain unresolved. For revenue, begin with promised goods or services, the commercial terms and any modifications. For leases, identify the arrangement, underlying asset, relevant dates and cash-flow evidence. For credit losses, inventory the asset population and support for assumptions. For business combinations, consolidation, income taxes, share-based payments, inventory, impairment, software and cash flows, create separate issue files rather than assuming that one workbook answers every topic.

## Research tracks

### Accounting Standards Codification

Nongovernmental U.S. financial reporting research.

- Which reporting entity, transaction and reporting period are in scope?
- Which topic, subtopic, section and paragraph apply?
- Is a paragraph current, pending, superseded or subject to transition relief?

Work product: A period-specific issue memo with source identifiers, assumptions and unresolved judgments.

### Accounting Standards Updates

Changes to the Codification and transition research.

- Which existing paragraphs change?
- What entity categories and interim/annual periods are affected?
- What adoption method, elections and disclosures require confirmation?

Work product: An amendment-to-Codification change log; do not treat a project announcement as an amended rule.

### Historical pronouncements and basis materials

Historical decisions and grandfathered transactions.

- What date and historical authority question require the older material?
- Has that material been superseded or incorporated elsewhere?

Work product: A historical-authority map that keeps explanatory history separate from governing text.

## Evidence and review gates

For each substantive conclusion, preserve the exact source identifier, edition or version, relevant reporting period, a precise locator, the fact pattern and any exceptions. Keep a direct quotation, an original paraphrase, a calculation and an assumption distinguishable. Record what source text was actually available and any unresolved access or rights gap.
