# AICPA audit and professional literature map

Editorial research outline · 2026-10-04 · No publisher standards text included

Select the engagement framework before collecting procedures. Record whether the engagement is an audit, review, compilation, preparation, examination or agreed-upon procedures engagement; the entity type; the subject matter; and the engagement period. Different service types require different research and do not carry the same reporting conclusion. These original prompts are not an AICPA standard, audit program or assurance opinion.

The initial file should contain the engagement terms, applicable reporting framework, intended users, management responsibilities, independence facts, quality-management responsibilities and material uncertainties about scope. For audits, connect a risk question to the planned response, evidence obtained and reporting consequence. For attestation, pin down criteria and subject matter. For preparation, compilation and review work, distinguish the agreed service and the associated report or communication. Escalate unresolved independence or engagement-acceptance questions to the authorized professional.

## Research tracks

### SAS / AU-C auditing standards

Nonissuer financial-statement audit research.

- Is the engagement within the nonissuer audit framework?
- How do risk assessment, responses, evidence and reporting connect?
- What current codified sections and amendments govern this engagement period?

Work product: An original audit research map and evidence-request checklist.

### SSAE / AT-C attestation standards

Attestation engagements.

- What subject matter, criteria, intended users and engagement type are specified?
- Which report form and assurance level are contemplated?
- What independence and evidence questions require confirmation?

Work product: An engagement-scoping worksheet separating examination, review and agreed-upon procedures research.

### SSARS / AR-C preparation, compilation and review standards

Unaudited financial information engagements.

- Which service was actually engaged?
- What management responsibilities and reporting language are relevant?
- Does another framework govern the entity or engagement?

Work product: A service-selection and deliverables checklist that does not imply an audit opinion.

### Quality management standards

Firm systems and engagement quality research.

- What quality objectives and risks are relevant to the firm?
- Who owns design, implementation, monitoring and remediation?
- How is the engagement-quality review requirement determined?

Work product: A responsibility/evidence matrix linking firm policies to demonstrable controls.

### Code of Professional Conduct

Ethics and independence research.

- Which professional role and covered relationships matter?
- What facts may create an independence or conflict concern?
- What authoritative interpretation and documentation are needed?

Work product: An independence fact register and escalation memo; no automated eligibility conclusion.

### Audit and accounting guides and practice aids

Industry-specific audit/accounting literature.

- Which edition, industry and entity class match the engagement?
- Which passages are authoritative requirements versus implementation commentary?
- What separate publisher permissions cover product use?

Work product: An industry issue list with source edition and authority classification.

## Evidence and review gates

For each substantive conclusion, preserve the exact source identifier, edition or version, relevant reporting period, a precise locator, the fact pattern and any exceptions. Keep a direct quotation, an original paraphrase, a calculation and an assumption distinguishable. Record what source text was actually available and any unresolved access or rights gap.
