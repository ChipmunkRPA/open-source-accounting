Editorial research outline · 2026-10-04 · No publisher standards text included

Begin with the governmental reporting entity and the statements being prepared. A single transaction may need to be understood in more than one reporting presentation. Record entity type, component-unit questions, fund type, reporting year and the relevant statement before researching a conclusion. These are original research prompts, not a reproduction of GASB literature.

A useful starting packet has an entity diagram, a trial-balance-to-statement map, major contracts, debt schedules, pension/OPEB information and a list of reporting changes. Keep fund-level work and government-wide work distinguishable. Investigate capital assets, leases, subscription arrangements, interfund activities, grants, pensions, OPEB and financial-reporting-model changes with separate applicability checks. Do not assume a FASAB federal-government requirement applies to state or local government reporting.

## Research tracks

### Governmental Accounting Research System

State and local governmental financial reporting.

- Is the reporting unit a government, component unit or another type of entity?
- Which fund and government-wide perspectives must be reconciled?
- What basis and measurement focus apply to each statement?

Work product: A reporting-entity and statement-level authority map.

### Statements, Interpretations and other pronouncements

Original issuances and their subsequent amendments.

- What is the issuance date versus the effective reporting period?
- Which statements amend the original requirement?
- Are leases, subscriptions, pensions, OPEB, capital assets or debt implicated?

Work product: A versioned applicability register organized by issue and reporting period.

### Implementation guidance

Application questions for governmental transactions.

- Which facts make the example relevant?
- Is the answer current after later pronouncements?
- Which judgment remains specific to the reporting entity?

Work product: An original fact-to-guidance worksheet with unresolved exceptions, not copied question-and-answer banks.

## Evidence and review gates

For each substantive conclusion, preserve the exact source identifier, edition or version, relevant reporting period, a precise locator, the fact pattern and any exceptions. Keep a direct quotation, an original paraphrase, a calculation and an assumption distinguishable. Record what source text was actually available and any unresolved access or rights gap.
