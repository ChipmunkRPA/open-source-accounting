# Ray Sang Annotation

## UNPUBLISHED educational draft checkpoint · Reader edition 0.1.2

These 15 original educational lessons are prepared for preservation in a public GitHub draft branch. **UNPUBLISHED describes editorial and production-release status, not confidentiality:** files saved in that branch are publicly readable under applicable rights and platform terms. These drafts have not been admitted to the product library, production manifest, retrieval index or Agent use.

The set contains seven ASC 105 foundational lessons followed by eight ASC 205 presentation lessons. Topic associations and lesson numbers form an editorial teaching sequence. They are not verified ASC paragraph mappings, exhaustive topic coverage or a reproduction of the Codification's order. Advancing to Topic 205 does not mean Topic 105 is complete.

## Accuracy and review limits

The explanations use original fictional scenarios. Current requirements, amendments, entity-specific exceptions and paragraph mapping remain unverified against authoritative material. Bounded editorial checks covered text, arithmetic, conditional assumptions and internal consistency. No professional review, professional-reliance approval, protected-corpus comparison, copyright clearance or legal fair-use conclusion is claimed. See [REVIEW-LIMITS.md](REVIEW-LIMITS.md).

The exact display brand is **Ray Sang Annotation**. It does not imply Ray Sang's personal review or publisher endorsement. The lessons are educational drafts, not authoritative accounting advice or compliance-ready reporting instructions.
