## Start from the actual correspondence

Obtain the exact comment letter, date, issuer and filing reference, relevant draft sections, prior correspondence and the response deadline actually stated or otherwise confirmed. A missing due date remains missing; do not infer one from a typical process. Identify who is responsible for factual verification, legal review and final submission.

The implemented workflow requires at least one ready document. That technical minimum does not establish that the letter, filing excerpt and complete supporting record are present. An incomplete packet must produce a visible gap rather than an invented SEC request.

## Separate each request from the answer proposed

Break each comment into distinct questions and requested actions. Link each component to the filing location and evidence needed. In the draft response, distinguish existing facts, interpretations, proposed future disclosure and new management commitments. A requested explanation is not permission to promise a policy change.

The SEC Financial Reporting Manual can help locate staff discussion but identifies itself as non-authoritative. Another issuer's correspondence is evidence of that particular exchange, not a safe harbor or a ruling for this issuer. The SEC reuse FAQ does not turn every quoted attachment into government-authored material or grant unrelated source operations. [sec-frm; sec-reuse]

## Original comment-tracker exercise

The following request is entirely fictional, not an SEC letter: “Explain the basis for the stated margin and clarify whether the two periods use the same definition.” It contains two questions. The provided memo answers only the calculation basis.

| Request component | Existing draft support | Missing work |
|---|---|---|
| Basis for the margin | A formula and one-period inputs | Verify input source, definition and calculation |
| Consistency between periods | No comparison supplied | Obtain both definitions and changes, if any |

A reply addressing one component does not answer both. A proposed sentence saying “we will revise every future filing” introduces a commitment not established by the packet. Mark it for the actual decision-maker rather than treating it as boilerplate.

For a separate original arithmetic check, stipulated revenue of USD 500000 and gross profit of USD 125000 produce 25%. That does not verify the underlying recognition, classification, permitted measure definition or the other period's comparability.

## Requested output and approval boundaries

Request a comment-to-response matrix, evidence requests, draft answer, proposed filing edits and unresolved representations or commitments. Preserve the actual question order and identify every answer still unsupported. Drafting may be useful even when a complete response cannot yet be supported.

The current catalogue explicitly limits this workflow to preparation: no filing, regulator contact or implication of regulator approval. A generated draft is not submission, and no source-code observation here proves completion of a real review process. Management and counsel must evaluate the actual facts and commitments; the authorized filer handles any eventual submission through a separate process.

## Version and deadline discipline

Keep the letter, filing and response draft identities together. Record the actual due date and owner without silently changing either. If evidence or proposed wording changes, recheck each affected representation and approval. Do not mark the SEC review closed because an internal task reached a completed state.
