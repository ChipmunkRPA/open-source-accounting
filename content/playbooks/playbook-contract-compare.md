Reading this playbook is free; executing new Agent work in the hosted product requires the annual Agent subscription.

## Required intake
Old and new signed versions; execution and effective dates; source-of-truth designation; reporting period.

## Execution plan
Compare normalized text but preserve original locators. Classify changes as pricing, duration, promises, acceptance, termination, payment, or wording-only. Do not infer legal effectiveness from a filename. Ask whether changes were enforceable before the reporting cutoff. Map changed facts to research issues without assuming every text difference changes accounting. Record deletion as a change, not as missing evidence.

## Expected deliverable
Old/new clause table; change summary; affected-accounting issue queue; unchanged assumptions; documents requiring legal confirmation.

## Stop or qualify the answer
Comparisons across unrelated customers; unsigned drafts mistaken for executed amendments; an assertion that text similarity proves equivalent rights.

## Reviewer handoff
Confirm the two documents represent the intended baseline and revision. Review numerical changes and dates. Accounting conclusions require a separate authority-supported evaluation.

## Data and rights boundaries
Use only the selected workspace and approved sources. Preserve citations and limitations in exported work. A subscription grants software access, not publisher rights. A source policy and a technical review are independent gates; this draft playbook is not automatically admitted to Agent evidence.
