## Intake and scope

Record the relevant risk, assertion, control narrative, owner, frequency, input population, stated precision, exception handling and reporting date. The workflow requires at least one ready document, but readiness is not evidence that a control is well designed or operated.

Separate what the procedure says should happen, what records claim happened and what was actually corroborated. A missing signature can be a documentation question without proving the control was absent; conversely, a signature alone does not prove that the described work was performed adequately.

## Original operation-log example

A fictional monthly control has three scheduled runs. The folder has one approved procedure, two execution files and a training record. Two execution files out of three scheduled runs is a 2/3 count, approximately 66.67%. It is not an operating-effectiveness score or proof that either recorded run was sufficient.

The third run's status remains unresolved until the evidence is investigated. The procedure and training documents are not extra monthly executions. Preserve the distinction between events, documents and control populations.

## Analyze without an automatic severity label

Identify the exposed population, possible failure paths, actual detected issues, other relevant activities and their evidence. Request the current criteria needed to evaluate the issue and any combined effects. Do not set a deficiency category from one dollar amount or presume that another described activity compensates for the gap.

For remediation, identify the change, implementation date, actual operation, exceptions and follow-up. Later records may inform an earlier-date assessment, but their true dates must remain visible. A redesigned procedure cannot be silently treated as proof of earlier successful operation.

## Requested output and professional decision

Request a risk-to-activity map, factual chronology, missing evidence, proposed design questions and a remediation/evaluation plan. Clearly label every proposed procedure as proposed. The catalogue describes this workflow as neither an operating-effectiveness test nor an automatic material-weakness determination.

A qualified reviewer applies the actual criteria and evaluates severity, remediation, aggregation and communications. The AI result supplies questions and organized evidence; it does not issue an ICFR opinion or approve a source.
