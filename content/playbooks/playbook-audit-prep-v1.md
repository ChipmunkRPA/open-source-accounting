## Establish the actual engagement

Record the engagement type, applicable framework, reporting period, area, risks, assertions and the people actually responsible for preparation and audit decisions. Do not assign an auditor's conclusion to management or make an AI-generated checklist appear to be a procedure already performed.

The code catalogue has no minimum document count for starting this workflow. That is an input rule, not proof that meaningful audit evidence exists. Identify the actual source packet and what remains missing before requesting analysis.

## Build an evidence-request register

For each question, record the population, relevant period, requested evidence, source system/document, expected reconciliation, actual owner if assigned and delivery status. Distinguish a requested report from a delivered report and a delivered report from one that has been tested.

Suggested procedures are planning ideas. Sample selection, evidence sufficiency, reliance and report conclusions require the appropriate engagement-specific professional decisions and current requirements. This guide supplies no sample-size rule or predetermined testing threshold.

## Original population example

An invented ledger extract lists 100 invoice IDs. A preparation folder contains 12 attachments, but two are stipulated exact copies of other attachments, leaving 10 distinct invoice IDs represented. The attachment-row ratio is 12/100 = 12%; the distinct-ID ratio is 10/100 = 10%.

Neither is a statistical assurance level, a population error estimate or proof that the invoices were audited. The packet does not establish the selection method, completeness, procedures performed or relevance of each attachment. Repeated IDs in real work require identity/context checking before classifying them as copies.

## Requested output and review

Request a risk/assertion/evidence map, a precise PBC request list, proposed checks, unresolved differences and the factual basis for each suggestion. Keep potential misstatements separate from confirmed ones. Preserve contrary evidence and limitations instead of ranking evidence by attachment count alone.

The responsible auditor determines the actual work and conclusions under the applicable standards. The product catalogue expressly excludes audit opinions, automatic sample sufficiency and claims that procedures were performed. A generated preparation pack does not change that boundary.
