## Intake and responsibilities

Record the reporting period, actual open issues, amounts/units, evidence, owners, dependencies and proposed entries. Identify who can decide each matter and who can authorize posting in the real ledger. Do not assign names or approvals that have not actually been given.

The workflow requires at least one ready document. Its catalogue expressly excludes posting entries, approving reconciliations or marking missing evidence complete. A task status in the documentation workspace does not change a ledger or close a professional review.

## Separate measurement, materiality and correction

For each possible error, preserve its origin, signed effects, affected periods and proposed correction. SAB 108 discusses current-period and accumulated effects including reversals; SAB 99 addresses quantitative and qualitative materiality. These SEC staff bulletins were inspected on October 4, 2026, with issuance dates September 13, 2006 and August 12, 1999 respectively. Neither decides the proper correction for an unknown entity. Current ASC correction requirements were not accessed. [sec-sab108; sec-sab99; fasb-asc]

## Original signed bridge

Stipulate a liability overstatement of USD 15000 at the start of the year, a new unsupported expense accrual of +USD 5000 and a proposed release through current-year expense of −USD 20000. The release column uses a negative sign.

Add signed effects: 15000 + 5000 + (−20000) = 0 ending liability error. Current-year expense error is 5000 + (−20000) = −15000. The closing balance can tie while the proposed entry shifts an earlier-years' error into current income. Subtracting the already negative release would be a sign mistake.

This example establishes only the stipulated bridge. It does not approve the entry, determine materiality or select restatement, revision or filing treatment.

## Build a dependency-aware issue register

Use one issue row per decision, with the evidence supporting the current status and the specific blocker. An amount can be calculated while its recognition is unresolved; a proposed entry can balance while its supporting facts are wrong. Distinguish those states rather than applying one green badge.

Record whether evidence was actually received, reviewed or merely requested. If an input changes, identify affected calculations and conclusions. Preserve prior versions so a reviewer can see why a previous conclusion changed.

## Requested output and release boundary

Request the issue register, signed bridges, evidence links, dependencies, draft entry alternatives and actual review status. A deadline does not make unresolved evidence complete. The controller or other authorized decision-maker must approve any real accounting action in the appropriate system.

The pack is documentation and preparation. It is not a general ledger, an audit opinion, permission to file or a source-rights grant.
