# Accounting policy drafting — Agent playbook

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original workflow guidance, not authoritative accounting advice. Primary ASC/other proprietary standard text is not bundled or directly verified. All scenarios are fictional. Confirm applicable authority, source permissions, and entity facts.

## Purpose and application status
This is an original procedure for the `policy_draft` Agent workflow. The code catalogue currently marks it **experimental / deployment-gated**. Publication of this playbook does not establish that the workflow, source coverage, or accounting conclusions have been professionally validated. Reading this playbook is free; executing new Agent work in the hosted product requires the annual Agent subscription.

## Required intake
Business model, accounting framework, entity scope, elected alternatives, chart-of-accounts context, approval owner.

## Execution plan
Write policy only after identifying which decisions management has actually made. Separate accounting principle, implementation procedure, control owner, and disclosure wording. Flag elections as undecided where necessary. Include examples clearly marked as illustrative and list policy exceptions requiring escalation. Retain a change log showing substantive differences from the prior approved policy.

## Expected deliverable
Policy draft; scope; decision register; procedures; control evidence; examples; approvals; unresolved authority and elections.

## Stop or qualify the answer
Invented management elections, automatic adoption of a peer policy, or tax treatment substituted for financial reporting.

## Reviewer handoff
Confirm decisions with management and verify authoritative support. A published internal policy does not resolve accounting contrary to governing requirements.

## Data and rights boundaries
Use only the selected workspace and approved sources. Preserve citations and limitations in exported work. A subscription grants software access, not publisher rights. A source policy and a technical review are independent gates; this draft playbook is not automatically admitted to Agent evidence.
