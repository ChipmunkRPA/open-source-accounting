## Select the intended versions in an explicit order

The inspected deterministic comparison accepts two to ten documents and compares each selected adjacent pair. It follows selected document order, not a verified chronological order. Confirm which file is the baseline and which amendments follow it. Retain document IDs, checksums and actual execution/effective-date evidence.

An unsigned draft, an executed amendment and an administrative export are different kinds of records. A filename or an upload timestamp does not decide which governs the relationship.

## What the current comparison actually supplies

The helper compares extracted text lines and returns bounded differences with pair identity and truncation indicators. It does not establish semantic equivalence, complete document coverage, visual amendments or operative legal rights. An empty difference can mean the selected extracts match; it cannot prove that the complete agreements are unchanged.

The helper limits input volume and divides the output allowance among adjacent pairs. If input is refused or output is truncated, narrow and inspect the scope instead of silently treating a partial comparison as complete. A text deletion should be preserved as a candidate difference; an unextracted page is a different kind of gap.

## Original three-version exercise

Invented monthly-fee text is USD 1000 in selected version A, USD 1100 in B and USD 1080 in C. The selected order A → B → C produces two adjacent numerical changes: +100, then −20. The overall A-to-C difference is +80. It is not +180, and two increases should not be inferred from the fact that both later fees exceed A.

Those differences describe the supplied numbers. They do not establish effective dates, which version was signed, collectability, consideration, an accounting modification or the amount to recognize. If B and C have conflicting dates, preserve the conflict rather than reorder them without an evidentiary basis.

## Turn differences into an issue list

For each candidate change, retain old/new locations, wording, units and timing. Group questions about pricing, term, promises, acceptance, termination and settlement, but do not assert a legal classification merely from vocabulary. Explain any normalization used by a separate human review; the current helper's output should not be described as a complete normalized semantic comparison.

Link changed facts to the accounting criteria that need research. Primary ASC text was not accessed for this playbook, so references to treatment or modification rules remain unverified research tasks. [fasb-asc]

## Requested output and review

Request the exact selected order, pairwise change record, truncated/excluded areas, factual contradictions and a prioritized accounting/legal question list. Verify important differences against the original files and confirm the intended baseline before interpreting them.

The comparison is useful preparation for analysis. It neither changes the documents nor determines rights, accounting treatment or approval to sign an amendment. A second model's similar wording is not evidence of equivalence.
