# 30 research and accounting study questions

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original workflow guidance, not authoritative accounting advice. Primary ASC/other proprietary standard text is not bundled or directly verified. All scenarios are fictional. Confirm applicable authority, source permissions, and entity facts.

## How to use this set
These 30 original questions test research habits and explicitly stipulated calculations. They are not a validated accounting benchmark, training corpus of licensed standards, or substitute for primary text. Reviewers should record corrections and accept or reject each answer before admitting it to evaluation gold data.

### qa-001: Does an ASC citation prove that the agent read the standard?
No. A citation identifies a location. Check the evidence record for whether primary text was actually accessed, which version was used, and whether it supports the proposition.

Related library item: `authority-and-period`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-002: Is the SEC Financial Reporting Manual itself authoritative?
The SEC describes it as non-authoritative. Use it as a research aid and resolve conflicts using applicable authoritative material.

Related library item: `authority-and-period`. References: sec-frm.

### qa-003: Can a peer disclosure establish the correct accounting?
Not by itself. It describes a company’s practice under its own facts. Establish comparability and verify the governing criteria separately.

Related library item: `authority-and-period`. References: sec-frm.

### qa-004: Does a nonrefundable implementation fee prove immediate revenue?
No conclusion follows from the billing label alone. Investigate whether a service transfers, whether promises are distinct, and the supported recognition pattern. Current ASC verification remains required.

Related library item: `saas-implementation`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-005: What evidence helps assess implementation services?
Collect the scope of work, deliverables, customer benefit, alternative providers, integration activities, acceptance terms, and when platform access begins. These are research inputs, not a decision rule.

Related library item: `saas-implementation`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-006: What is a relative-SSP allocation with an agreed 80:20 ratio and 90,000 price?
Under those stipulated arithmetic assumptions: 72,000 and 18,000. This does not establish distinct obligations, valid standalone prices, or the absence of an allocation exception.

Related library item: `case-ssp-allocation`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-007: Does invoicing identify the revenue date?
No. Treat invoice dates as billing evidence; recognition needs a separate analysis of the applicable criteria and facts.

Related library item: `revenue-research-map`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-008: Can one generic percentage allocation be used for every contract?
Not on that basis alone. Establish the applicable method, support the inputs, and evaluate whether an exception affects the allocation.

Related library item: `revenue-research-map`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-009: How should a payment to a customer be researched?
Investigate the contractual purpose, recipient relationship, any distinct good or service received, pricing evidence, and Topic 606 references. Do not assume that a marketing label resolves classification.

Related library item: `customer-payments`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-010: What additional issue arises with equity paid to a customer?
The FASB has announced changes at the intersection of Topics 606 and 718. Verify the actual applicable update, effective dates, and award terms rather than applying a cash-payment template mechanically.

Related library item: `customer-payments`. References: fasb-customer-equity.

### qa-011: Does a contract calling an arrangement a service exclude a lease?
The label is not enough. Research the identified asset, substitution rights, benefits, and direction-of-use facts against current applicable criteria.

Related library item: `lease-identification`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-012: Does a three-year lease schedule establish classification?
No. It is a calculation under assumptions. Classification, term, components, commencement, and rate each require their own support.

Related library item: `lease-measurement`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-013: What is the PV of three year-end 10,000 payments at 6%?
Approximately 26,730.12 using 10,000/(1.06)^t for t = 1, 2, 3. Timing, rate, and payment inputs are stipulated for the exercise.

Related library item: `case-lease-present-value`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-014: Why might a displayed schedule differ by one cent?
Displayed figures may be rounded from higher-precision balances. Define a documented rounding method and reconcile the final amount; never conceal an unexplained difference.

Related library item: `case-lease-present-value`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-015: Is an award’s headline grant value enough to book compensation?
No. Assemble terms, grant and service facts, vesting conditions, settlement provisions, modifications, and valuation evidence before assessing treatment.

Related library item: `share-based-award-intake`. References: fasb-asc, sec-sab14.

### qa-016: Does a modification automatically preserve the original expense pattern?
Do not assume that. Obtain both award versions, dates, approvals, valuation inputs, and the relevant current guidance.

Related library item: `share-based-award-intake`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-017: Can a legal-loss reserve be classified using an internal label alone?
No. Separate recognition, measurement, disclosure, and current/noncurrent presentation. Determine the nature and timing of the obligation and applicable presentation guidance.

Related library item: `loss-contingencies`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-018: Should income-tax uncertainty and other loss contingencies be treated as identical?
No. Identify the scope first. An income-tax issue may belong under Topic 740; a non-income-tax or other contingency may require a different framework. Verify rather than generalize.

Related library item: `loss-contingencies`. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-019: Is 5% an automatic immateriality rule?
No. SEC SAB 99 rejects exclusive reliance on a numerical threshold. Qualitative context matters.

Related library item: `materiality`. References: sec-sab99.

### qa-020: Is a small amount always immaterial?
No. Analyze the surrounding facts and qualitative effects, not just the amount in isolation.

Related library item: `materiality`. References: sec-sab99.

### qa-021: Why calculate errors in more than one way?
SAB 108 addresses both current-period and accumulated balance-sheet effects. Document both perspectives and then evaluate materiality and correction separately.

Related library item: `error-measurement`. References: sec-sab108.

### qa-022: If a fictional error adds 12,000 per year for three years, what is accumulated?
36,000 at the third year-end, with 12,000 from the third year and 24,000 accumulated earlier, under the exercise assumptions. No materiality conclusion is implied.

Related library item: `case-accumulated-error`. References: sec-sab108.

### qa-023: Does an ICFR deficiency require an actual misstatement?
Not necessarily. AS 2201’s severity analysis concerns the potential for misstatement, not just errors already found.

Related library item: `icfr-deficiencies`. References: pcaob-as2201.

### qa-024: Does a compensating control automatically eliminate a deficiency?
No. Evaluate whether the control operates at sufficient precision and addresses the relevant risk, with evidence of its effectiveness.

Related library item: `icfr-deficiencies`. References: pcaob-as2201.

### qa-025: Does installing a new control prove remediation?
No. Separate design, implementation, and demonstrated operation. The assessment must fit the relevant reporting date.

Related library item: `case-control-remediation`. References: pcaob-as2201.

### qa-026: Is more evidence always better evidence?
Quantity does not cure every quality problem. AS 1105 distinguishes sufficiency from appropriateness, including relevance and reliability.

Related library item: `electronic-audit-evidence`. References: pcaob-as1105.

### qa-027: What should be checked for an external electronic report supplied by management?
Examine its source and processing path, and determine what testing of the information or relevant controls is needed under applicable requirements.

Related library item: `electronic-audit-evidence`. References: pcaob-electronic-policy.

### qa-028: Can explanatory notes cure every misleading non-GAAP measure?
No. SEC staff interpretations identify circumstances in which explanation does not make an otherwise misleading measure acceptable. Evaluate the measure itself and applicable presentation rules.

Related library item: `non-gaap-review`. References: sec-nongaap.

### qa-029: When does the 2024 Yellow Book generally apply?
Financial audits, attestation engagements, and reviews of financial statements for periods beginning on or after December 15, 2025; performance audits beginning on or after that date. Check early implementation and special quality-management timing notes separately.

Related library item: `yellow-book-scope`. References: gao-yellowbook.

### qa-030: Does paying for Agent license proprietary accounting standards?
No. It unlocks application workflows under the hosted-service plan. Source access and reuse permissions remain separate; this repository does not include the ASC.

Related library item: `source-onboarding-template`. References: fasb-asc, pcaob-terms.
