# Expanded study questions: evidence and model selection

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Twenty-eight new questions extend the original thirty; these are educational drafts, not adjudicated benchmark answers.

## qa-031 — What is the first research step for cash flows?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Obtain beginning and ending bank reconciliations, restricted-cash schedules, the cash general ledger, debt and lease rollforwards, acquisition records, and foreign-currency movements. Record the reporting entity, consolidation scope, currency, period, and which accounts are inside the cash reconciliation. A bank transfer between two included accounts should not be counted twice as an external receipt and payment in the working data.

## qa-032 — What should the Agent deliver for the cash flows workbook?

Produce a reconciliation with unresolved amounts separately identified, a classification decision log, citations requiring primary-text verification, and disclosure questions. The reviewer should be able to trace every significant line to transactions and reproduce the tie-out. The Agent must stop short of declaring compliance when the opening balance, acquisition scope, or noncash population is missing. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-033 — What is the first research step for receivable losses?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Identify the instruments and determine which measurement model needs research before importing historical loss percentages. Obtain invoice-level balances, contractual terms, write-offs, recoveries, credit notes, disputes, subsequent collections, customer concentrations, and credit enhancements. Separate credit risk from pricing concessions, billing errors, revenue reversals, and tax corrections.

## qa-034 — What should the Agent deliver for the credit losses workbook?

Deliver a validated population, segmentation rationale, assumption register, sensitivity table, rollforward, and open judgment log. The reviewer should challenge data leakage from later periods and optimistic assumptions. The Agent may compute stipulated scenarios but must not independently set a probability of default or claim that a calculated amount satisfies GAAP. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-035 — What is the first research step for inventory?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Obtain inventory by item and location, count instructions, count results, ownership terms, goods in transit, consignment arrangements, production records, cost layers, overhead allocations, aging, and subsequent selling information. Identify whether the inventory is manufactured, purchased, work in process, or held for a customer. Record the costing method and the scope of any specialized industry guidance.

## qa-036 — What should the Agent deliver for the inventory workbook?

Produce linked quantity, ownership, costing, and valuation schedules. Mark estimates and missing third-party confirmations separately. The reviewer should reconcile reserve movements and examine whether management offset unrelated gains and losses. The Agent should identify gaps and calculate explicitly assumed scenarios, not invent sales forecasts or substitute for inventory observation. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-037 — What is the first research step for impairment?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. List the assets being evaluated, carrying amounts, allocation of associated balances, reporting units or asset groups proposed by management, useful lives, held-for-sale assertions, and relevant acquisition history. Identify goodwill, finite-lived intangibles, indefinite-lived intangibles, tangible long-lived assets, and assets governed by other guidance. Different labels may lead to different testing paths.

## qa-038 — What should the Agent deliver for the impairment workbook?

Deliver a model map, evidence chronology, carrying-amount tie-out, forecast version register, sensitivities, and conditional conclusions. The reviewer should test whether favorable assumptions are consistent across budgets, disclosures, and the impairment calculation. The Agent must not label a valuation as independently appraised or professionally reviewed. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-039 — What is the first research step for fair value?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Identify the instrument or asset, unit of account, relevant market participants, measurement date, transaction restrictions, valuation purpose, and the accounting standard requiring or permitting fair value. Gather quoted prices, transaction evidence, market data, model documentation, and management adjustments. Research scope rather than assuming that every internally estimated value is an ASC 820 measurement.

## qa-040 — What should the Agent deliver for the fair value workbook?

Produce the valuation rationale, input lineage, unresolved quotes, assumption sensitivities, and required specialist questions. The Agent may test arithmetic and consistency but must not claim to have performed an independent specialist valuation. The reviewer should identify which inputs most affect the result and whether available contradictory information was addressed. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-041 — What is the first research step for acquisitions?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Obtain the complete purchase agreement, schedules, closing statement, side letters, financing agreements, ownership chart, service arrangements, valuation reports, and board approvals. Identify what was acquired and what activities and resources transferred. Determine which business-versus-asset and acquirer-identification questions require authoritative research.

## qa-042 — What should the Agent deliver for the business combinations workbook?

Deliver a transaction map, issue register, payment reconciliation, valuation requests, draft entry alternatives, and a disclosure checklist. The reviewer should examine omitted schedules and related agreements before approving scope. The Agent must not allocate an unsupported residual to goodwill merely to make entries balance. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-043 — What is the first research step for consolidation?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Obtain ownership records, governing documents, investor agreements, management contracts, financing arrangements, guarantees, options, related-party relationships, and amendments. Identify the purpose and activities of each legal entity. Document direct and indirect interests rather than relying only on a capitalization table.

## qa-044 — What should the Agent deliver for the consolidation workbook?

Produce a legal-entity map, clause-to-criterion matrix, evidence gaps, alternative-model discussion, and reassessment triggers. The reviewer should examine the entire arrangement, including guarantees outside the main agreement. The Agent should stop when material governing documents are absent and should never substitute generated summaries for legal interpretation of disputed rights. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-045 — What is the first research step for income taxes?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Gather pretax book income by legal entity and jurisdiction, tax returns or provision workpapers, permanent and temporary differences, tax bases, loss and credit carryforwards, expiry schedules, uncertain-position analyses, and changes in law. Identify whether a levy is an income tax within scope before selecting the accounting model. Do not classify payroll, sales, or marketplace taxes solely by the name of the department managing them.

## qa-046 — What should the Agent deliver for the income taxes workbook?

Deliver a reconciled provision file, evidence register, open law/interpretation questions, draft disclosure questions, and reviewer sign-offs. Tax-law conclusions require appropriately qualified review. The Agent can cross-check totals and missing documentation but must not invent enacted rates or treat an ambiguous tax as automatically subject to ASC 740. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-047 — What is the first research step for foreign currency?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Identify each entity, its local books currency, asserted functional currency, presentation currency, transaction currencies, and consolidation path. Obtain transaction dates, settlement dates, rate sources, historical-rate records, monetary/nonmonetary classifications proposed by management, and changes in the economic environment. Record whether a proposed functional-currency change has actually been approved.

## qa-048 — What should the Agent deliver for the foreign currency workbook?

Deliver a functional-currency fact file, rate-source register, rollforward, error-versus-policy analysis, and draft entries with explicit assumptions. The reviewer should trace every exchange difference to its cause. The Agent may perform unit-checked arithmetic but must not assign functional currency or post exchange entries autonomously. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-049 — What is the first research step for going concern?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Record the financial-statement date, expected issuance or availability date, management's assessment period, audit-report date, reporting framework, and audit regime. Obtain forecasts, cash restrictions, debt maturities, covenant calculations, financing agreements, supplier terms, board plans, and support commitments. Do not collapse these dates into a single generic twelve-month window.

## qa-050 — What should the Agent deliver for the going concern workbook?

Deliver a timeline, documented assumptions, funding-status matrix, scenario output, disclosure questions, and separate management/auditor responsibility maps. Escalate unsupported plans and date gaps. The reviewer must verify authoritative criteria and evaluate judgment; the Agent must not issue an assurance conclusion or predict solvency. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-051 — What is the first research step for subsequent events?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Collect the reporting date, issuance or availability date, audit-report date, and the date each event was discovered. Obtain legal updates, board minutes, financing agreements, settlements, customer failures, acquisitions, major casualty information, and relevant subsequent financial results. Distinguish when a condition existed from when someone learned of it.

## qa-052 — What should the Agent deliver for the subsequent events workbook?

Deliver an event register, candidate accounting treatment, factual uncertainties, proposed disclosure text only where justified, and a final update checklist. The reviewer should check completeness of the search period and whether contradictory evidence was omitted. The Agent must not mark the subsequent-event review complete when the evaluation endpoint remains unknown. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-053 — What is the first research step for related parties?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Obtain the ownership and management structure, beneficial-owner information available to the entity, conflict declarations, board minutes, intercompany balances, guarantees, unusual customers or vendors, and transaction agreements. Record the scope of the applicable definition before classifying a party. Family, affiliate, and management relationships require facts, not name matching alone.

## qa-054 — What should the Agent deliver for the related parties workbook?

Produce a dated party map, ledger reconciliation, missing confirmations, disclosure matrix, and exceptions requiring review. Protect personal data and use role-based access for sensitive relationship details. The Agent must not infer hidden personal relationships from names, social media, or unsupported speculation. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-055 — What is the first research step for software costs?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Obtain project purpose, intended users, hosting and licensing arrangements, customer access rights, project phases, approvals, development milestones, time records, vendor invoices, abandoned features, and enhancement descriptions. Establish the reporting period and whether recently issued amendments are adopted. The label cloud or AI does not independently determine the accounting model.

## qa-056 — What should the Agent deliver for the software costs workbook?

Deliver an arrangement map, cost/activity matrix, unsupported allocations, chronology, candidate accounting policies, and amortization assumptions. The reviewer should reconcile policy with approved project evidence. The Agent must not turn a project manager's retrospective estimate into verified time records. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.

## qa-057 — What is the first research step for debt and equity-linked instruments?

Establish the scope and facts before selecting a model. Obtain signed agreements, amendments, side letters, conversion terms, warrants, puts and calls, redemption provisions, covenant calculations, guarantees, fees, settlement alternatives, and board approvals. Record issuer and holder perspectives separately. A SAFE, note, preferred share, or warrant label is only an intake category.

## qa-058 — What should the Agent deliver for the debt and equity workbook?

Deliver a financing map, full-term extraction, candidate accounting paths, missing clauses, calculation requests, and proposed entries only after the model is established. The reviewer should challenge assumptions about settlement control and scope. Legal enforceability and specialist valuation questions need appropriate human review. Any missing primary-text verification must remain visible.
