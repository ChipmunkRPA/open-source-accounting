# Important U.S. federal tax case law

Version 1.0.0 · October 4, 2026

[Read the 32 original case briefs](BRIEFS.md), inspect the [structured catalog](case-catalog.json), or browse the [14-topic index](topic-index.json).

The selection contains 21 Supreme Court decisions, seven appellate decisions from five circuits, and four Tax Court decisions. Topics include income and realization, basis and debt, capital versus ordinary treatment, deductions and capitalization, substance and step transactions, assignment of income, entity tax, transfer pricing, administrative review, procedure and penalties. Decisions at different levels of the same litigation are separate entries; this is a representative starter selection, not comprehensive coverage.

Each brief gives the case, citation, docket, court, decision date, material facts, issue, holding, reasoning, authority level, application limits and a dated, bounded history check. The prose and arrangement are original CC BY 4.0 educational material under [the content license](../LICENSE.md). No Westlaw/Lexis headnotes or proprietary key numbers are used.

## Important status warnings

- Loper Bright overruled Chevron, while preserving ordinary statutory stare decisis for prior holdings. Mayo and Altera are flagged for that methodological change
- Moore addresses attribution of entity-realized income and leaves the broader constitutional realization question unresolved
- The Tax Court decisions in Farhy and 3M were reversed. 3M’s later Supreme Court-review status remains unresolved beyond the verified 2026 filing extension
- Safdieh (Second Circuit, 2026) agrees with Farhy on §6038(b) assessment. Its deadline to seek certiorari was extended to November 23, 2026; that extension is not merits review
- Maniktala (Eighth Circuit, August 2026) joins Second, Third and Sixth Circuit decisions on potential §6213(a) tolling. The catalog is not a complete current circuit map
- After the Supreme Court remand in Boechler, the Eighth Circuit affirmed denial of actual tolling on August 10, 2026
- Amazon concerns a historical transfer-pricing regime. Green Valley does not eliminate disclosure duties imposed by later regulations

Recheck the controlling court, applicable period, current statute and later history before relying on any case.
