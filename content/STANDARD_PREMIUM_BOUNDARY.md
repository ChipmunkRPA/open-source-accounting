# Standard content and Premium service

## Standard: free educational and source-reading material

Original guides, fictional worked cases, templates, study questions and
educational workflow descriptions retain their stated CC BY 4.0 license. Public
source-reference metadata, permitted government text and their attribution keep
their own rights and edition limits. Free general chat and public reading remain
free. A template does not become proprietary merely because someone uses it in
a paid workflow.

The Standard publication repository is
[Open Source Accounting](https://github.com/ChipmunkRPA/open-source-accounting).
Only deliberately selected public content and free-client changes belong there.
Reference links do not license proprietary standards or publisher commentary.

## Premium: hosted Agent execution and private implementation

Premium covers the hosted Agent service and its private orchestration, prompts,
backend, paid-workflow interfaces and operational implementation. The service
price remains US$89.99 per year; this is not a copyright charge on downstream
reuse of Standard CC BY content. No monthly or unlimited allowance is promised.

Premium implementation stays in the separate private repository. Public
publication must not copy private source history, operational reports, tests,
credentials, user documents or licensed corpora. A private application may
consume an explicitly versioned Standard content release while preserving its
license, attribution, content hashes and review limits.

## Review and source rights

AI editorial checks and professional sign-off are distinct records. Neither a
paid subscription nor an AI review gives permission to acquire or reproduce
third-party material. Current source rights, exact content versions, reporting
period and workspace access still control what may be used in Agent execution.
