# Debt and equity-linked instruments: read the complete terms

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Build a term-level research file for financing arrangements without classifying instruments from their names.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 470, ASC 480, ASC 815, and applicable presentation guidance**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Obtain signed agreements, amendments, side letters, conversion terms, warrants, puts and calls, redemption provisions, covenant calculations, guarantees, fees, settlement alternatives, and board approvals. Record issuer and holder perspectives separately. A SAFE, note, preferred share, or warrant label is only an intake category.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Create a term matrix covering principal, interest, maturity, contingencies, settlement medium, who controls settlement, adjustments to conversion or exercise terms, and triggers for acceleration or redemption. Reconcile cash proceeds and fees to the closing records and ledger. Link every significant term to a document locator.

## Analysis workstream

Research classification, embedded-feature analysis, separate instruments, measurement, presentation, and subsequent modifications under the applicable guidance. Map contingencies and settlement rights to the criteria instead of relying on a checklist that ignores exceptions. Reassess the analysis when terms change; a modification and an extinguishment are not interchangeable labels to be selected based only on management preference.

## A failure mode to test

A financing can change economically through a side letter even when the main document is unchanged. A conversion feature that appears simple may depend on future financing prices or other variables. The Agent should expose such dependencies, not force a debt-versus-equity answer from incomplete terms.

## Deliverable and review gate

Deliver a financing map, full-term extraction, candidate accounting paths, missing clauses, calculation requests, and proposed entries only after the model is established. The reviewer should challenge assumptions about settlement control and scope. Legal enforceability and specialist valuation questions need appropriate human review.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
