# FERC research: connect the ledger, authority and reporting destination

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original research aid, not FERC instructions, an accounting determination or a completed regulatory return. All numerical inputs are fictional.

## Define the assignment

Write the legal entity, jurisdiction basis, industry, reporting period, requested return and proposed accounting question. Identify whether the work concerns electric utilities, gas, oil pipelines or centralized services before selecting a source package. This guide does not determine who must file or prescribe a system of accounts. Do not reuse an electric-company pilot for another industry without a separately verified basis.

Keep three linked records: the organization's ledger facts, the applicable regulatory instructions, and the reporting destination. An account title resembling a form label does not prove a valid mapping. Obtain the exact account/instruction locator and exact form schedule, row/column or taxonomy concept for the period. Leave the destination unresolved when the authority is missing.

## Prepare the source package

For every rule, order, instruction or staff explanation, record the issuer, authority class, docket or identifier, exact edition and amendments, original artifact hash, locator and separate issued/available/effective/retrieved dates. Resolve current, superseded, rescinded and historical-use status. A link remaining on an index is not an applicability decision. Staff explanation, company filing practice and governing instructions must remain distinguishable in the evidence file.

Inspect the original instructions and any applicable orders before asserting treatment of construction costs, financing costs, depreciation, retirements or regulatory assets/liabilities. A proposed adjustment labeled “regulatory” does not establish recognition or measurement. Retain its facts, calculation, alternative treatments and unresolved criteria. The current pilot has not obtained the bodies needed to settle those questions.

## Reconcile without concealing differences

Freeze the ledger export with its date, entity, currency, unit scale, sign convention and hash. Record beginning balance, movements and ending balance; trace them to supporting records. Keep proposed bridge items separate from accepted adjustments. Show any remaining difference against a separately sourced target; never use an unexplained plug merely to force agreement.

For each mapping, distinguish gross balance, contra balance and net presentation. Cross-foot both entity and account groupings. A consolidated zero difference can hide offsetting entity errors; a matching net number can hide materially different gross balances. These are arithmetic failure modes, not a determination of materiality or prescribed presentation.

## Exceptions and review questions

An acquisition or disposal may change which entity owns a balance. Intercompany entries can match in total yet refer to different periods. A rounded report can differ from a whole-currency ledger. A prior-period correction may require investigation beyond the current bridge. Resolve each with facts and the relevant instructions, rather than silently netting or carrying forward an old mapping.

A specialist should decide whether each proposed treatment and reporting destination is supported for the entity and period. The reviewer must identify exact source and workpaper revisions, scope, exceptions and date. Source-operation permission, workspace access and professional review are separate. Completing a reconciliation neither posts journals nor files a return; both remain separately controlled actions.
