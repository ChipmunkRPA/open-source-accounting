# Build an authority and reporting-period map

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## The issue
An answer can cite real documents and still be wrong because it uses the wrong framework, period, entity category, or source type. Establish those boundaries before researching the transaction.

## Research workflow
Create a cover sheet containing the legal entity, consolidated or separate reporting scope, framework, issuer status, engagement type, period beginning and ending dates, and any early-adoption elections. Record unknowns rather than filling them with a model's assumptions. Keep “guidance applicable to the period” separate from “information available when management made the decision.”

The SEC describes its Financial Reporting Manual as non-authoritative; the underlying authoritative material controls in a conflict. Therefore a useful manual reference must not be displayed as though it were the governing standard. The same product distinction applies to an original teaching article in this library. [sec-frm]

## Evidence map
Use one row per proposition, not one bibliography for the entire memo:

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Proposition | The specific requirement or factual premise |
| Authority | Standard, rule, staff interpretation, company example, or commentary |
| Locator | Actual paragraph/section/page from the material examined |
| Version | Issue date, applicable amendments, and reporting-period fit |
| Access | Primary text reviewed, secondary discussion, or reference only |
| Counterevidence | Sources or facts that point to a different result |
| Reviewer action | Verify, resolve, accept with limitation, or reject |

A company's disclosure is useful for researching how that company explained its facts. It does not, by itself, establish a generally acceptable treatment. A search result is also not a substitute for examining the source passage and necessary context. These are the library's editorial safeguards, not assertions that a particular regulator approved the workflow.

## Example
A preparer requests a 2024 memo and finds a guide updated in 2026. Do not assume the new guide describes the rules applicable in 2024. Record the amendment history as an open verification task and preserve the date on the later article. Until applicability is resolved, describe the conclusion as conditional.

## Agent output and stop conditions
Produce a one-page authority map, a missing-facts list, and a research plan. Stop short of a definitive conclusion when the key paragraph cannot be accessed or applicability cannot be determined. The system may explain the missing step; it must not fabricate a quotation or a professional-review badge.

## Sources and verification

- [FASB Accounting Standards Codification](https://asc.fasb.org/) — standard; Topic and paragraph identifiers are research references. Current Codification text was not independently accessed for this starter pack.
- [Financial Reporting Manual](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-corporation-finance/financial-reporting-manual) — staff guidance; Landing page expressly describes the manual as non-authoritative. Not a substitute for the underlying rules.
- [AS 1105 — Audit Evidence](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS1105) — auditing standard; Selected evidence quality and company-provided information provisions; version applicability must be checked.
