# Income taxes: reconcile the provision without mixing tax regimes

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Build a jurisdiction-specific accounting evidence file and keep income-tax accounting distinct from unrelated tax obligations.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 740**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Gather pretax book income by legal entity and jurisdiction, tax returns or provision workpapers, permanent and temporary differences, tax bases, loss and credit carryforwards, expiry schedules, uncertain-position analyses, and changes in law. Identify whether a levy is an income tax within scope before selecting the accounting model. Do not classify payroll, sales, or marketplace taxes solely by the name of the department managing them.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Reconcile statutory-to-effective-rate differences and deferred-tax movements separately. Build temporary-difference schedules with opening book/tax basis, current-year movement, reversal pattern, applicable rate, and closing basis. Document consolidation eliminations and allocation between income, equity, and other reporting components as separate research questions.

## Analysis workstream

For deferred-tax realization, assemble both supporting and contrary evidence and connect projected taxable income to the right jurisdiction, character, and time horizon. For uncertain positions, identify the unit being assessed, legal support, assumptions about examination, recognition and measurement questions, and interest or penalty policy. Do not use the cash-tax forecast as a substitute for the accounting analysis.

## A failure mode to test

A deferred-tax asset and an income-tax receivable can have different origins and evidence needs. A loss forecast does not mechanically determine every valuation allowance, nor does a current profit prove every carryforward will be usable. Preserve jurisdiction-specific limitations and uncertainties rather than netting them away.

## Deliverable and review gate

Deliver a reconciled provision file, evidence register, open law/interpretation questions, draft disclosure questions, and reviewer sign-offs. Tax-law conclusions require appropriately qualified review. The Agent can cross-check totals and missing documentation but must not invent enacted rates or treat an ambiguous tax as automatically subject to ASC 740.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
