# Fair value: document the market and the significant inputs

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Distinguish the reason for fair-value measurement from the valuation method and the reliability of its inputs.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 820 and the standard requiring the measurement**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Identify the instrument or asset, unit of account, relevant market participants, measurement date, transaction restrictions, valuation purpose, and the accounting standard requiring or permitting fair value. Gather quoted prices, transaction evidence, market data, model documentation, and management adjustments. Research scope rather than assuming that every internally estimated value is an ASC 820 measurement.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Prepare an input inventory showing source, observation date, market, adjustments, currency, and reviewer. Distinguish directly observed data from interpolated, adjusted, or unobservable inputs. Reconcile the population valued to the accounting records. Preserve stale or conflicting quotes as exceptions instead of silently selecting the most favorable value.

## Analysis workstream

Explain why the proposed valuation technique fits the asset and available information. Check calibration, consistency between forecast units and discount rates, and the effect of significant adjustments. Document hierarchy and disclosure questions based on the actual inputs to the complete measurement, not just the name of the model. An audit-estimate review may also examine management's process, independent expectations, or relevant subsequent transactions; verify applicable auditing requirements separately.

## A failure mode to test

A quote is evidence, not a complete valuation conclusion. It may refer to a different instrument, a restricted interest, an inactive market, or a date outside the measurement period. A model that produces many decimal places does not overcome a weak input population.

## Deliverable and review gate

Produce the valuation rationale, input lineage, unresolved quotes, assumption sensitivities, and required specialist questions. The Agent may test arithmetic and consistency but must not claim to have performed an independent specialist valuation. The reviewer should identify which inputs most affect the result and whether available contradictory information was addressed.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
