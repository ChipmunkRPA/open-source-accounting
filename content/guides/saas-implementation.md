# SaaS implementation fees: investigate the promise before the price

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. **Primary ASC text was not accessed.** Paragraph references are verification tasks, not evidence that current authoritative wording was reviewed. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## Decision to investigate
A line item called implementation does not establish the unit of account. Begin with what the customer receives, the relationship between that output and the hosted service, and the vendor's obligations if the work fails. Candidate references include ASC 606-10-25-17 through 25-22 and, for upfront fees, the relevant implementation guidance in Topic 606. These references need current primary-text verification. [fasb-asc]

## Fact questions
Ask the operations team to describe the work without accounting labels. Does the provider migrate data, supply a usable training deliverable, configure its own system, customize software, integrate multiple outputs, or perform several of these activities? Can the customer retain or use an output independently? Can another provider perform the service? What contractual and practical dependencies remain?

Record whether the customer obtains any output while implementation is underway. Separately record whether platform access is available before completion and why. A technical dependency is evidence to investigate, not an automatic accounting answer.

## Two comparison scenarios
In Scenario A, the vendor supplies a documented migration package that the customer can retain and use in another environment. In Scenario B, the vendor performs internal activation work and the customer receives no output apart from subsequent access. These different facts justify different research paths. Neither outcome should be selected merely because both invoices say “implementation.”

## Analysis matrix
For each promised activity, document the proposed output, evidence that the customer can benefit, interaction with other promises, acceptance terms, and alternative explanations. Test the current distinctness criteria against the facts rather than reproducing a standard paragraph. Where no separate transfer is supported, investigate how the associated fee relates to the actual promised services and any renewal-related questions.

## Model answer boundaries
The agent can say: “The present evidence does not establish that implementation is a separate performance obligation. Obtain the operational deliverable description and verify the current criteria.” It should not say: “Implementation is always deferred” or “Separate invoicing proves separate recognition.”

## Reviewer package
Attach the signed contract, scope of work, implementation delivery evidence, interview notes, candidate authority references, and a fact-sensitive conclusion. Show exactly which missing fact could reverse the proposed treatment. Recognition timing and price allocation remain separate analyses even after identifying the promises.
