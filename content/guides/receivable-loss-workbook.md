# Receivable losses: turn aging data into an evidence file

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Prepare the inputs and judgments for a credit-loss analysis without treating an aging matrix as an automatic GAAP conclusion.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 326 and the applicable receivable guidance**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Identify the instruments and determine which measurement model needs research before importing historical loss percentages. Obtain invoice-level balances, contractual terms, write-offs, recoveries, credit notes, disputes, subsequent collections, customer concentrations, and credit enhancements. Separate credit risk from pricing concessions, billing errors, revenue reversals, and tax corrections.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Reconcile the population to the general ledger and document the extraction date, open-item logic, currencies, deleted records, and joins. Reconcile changes in an allowance using opening balance, provision, write-offs, recoveries, acquisitions, and other movements. Do not let a modeling tool quietly remove disputed or overdue items as outliers.

## Analysis workstream

Propose pools using documented risk characteristics and ask whether specific exposures need separate evaluation. Trace historical loss measurements to both numerator and exposure denominator. Document why historical experience is relevant, what current information changes it, and how forecasts and any reversion assumptions enter the method. Keep analyst overlays distinct from mechanically generated results and retain evidence for each adjustment. Obtain the current authoritative requirements before selecting a permissible method.

## A failure mode to test

If two analysts produce the same allowance using different aging populations, the match is not corroboration. One may exclude new customers while the other excludes disputed balances. Similarly, a later receipt may be evidence about a particular exposure without proving the entire remaining pool is collectible. Investigate the customer, timing, and payment application.

## Deliverable and review gate

Deliver a validated population, segmentation rationale, assumption register, sensitivity table, rollforward, and open judgment log. The reviewer should challenge data leakage from later periods and optimistic assumptions. The Agent may compute stipulated scenarios but must not independently set a probability of default or claim that a calculated amount satisfies GAAP.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
