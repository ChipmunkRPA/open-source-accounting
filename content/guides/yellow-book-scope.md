# Government auditing: determine the Yellow Book engagement and version

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## Scope
GAO's Yellow Book addresses government auditing, including financial audits, attestation engagements, and performance audits. Do not assume that every engagement involving a nonprofit or government-related party has identical requirements; identify the law, award, contract, or other basis requiring the work. [gao-yellowbook]

## Applicability note
The 2024 revision applies to financial audits, attestation engagements, and reviews for periods beginning on or after December 15, 2025, and to performance audits beginning on or after that date; early implementation is permitted. The current GAO landing page also contains a special federal audit-organization quality-management timing note. Check that note instead of extending a general deadline indiscriminately. [gao-yellowbook; gao-yellowbook-2024]

## Intake worksheet
Record the engagement type, subject entity, period, governing requirement, applicable other professional standards, prior version used, and early-implementation decision. Separate the engagement's effective date from organization-level quality-management dates. The December 2025 GAO FAQ is a companion practice aid, not a substitute for the standard. [gao-qm-faq]

## Original planning scenario
A firm begins a performance audit during 2026 but is also completing a financial audit for an earlier period. The team should not choose the applicable edition solely from the report-signing year. Build separate applicability records for each engagement and verify all transition considerations.

## Evidence and review
Create a checklist covering independence questions, competence and training evidence, engagement supervision, reporting requirements, and organization-level quality-management considerations appropriate to the engagement. These are planning categories, not a claim that this starter pack contains every required procedure or requirement.
