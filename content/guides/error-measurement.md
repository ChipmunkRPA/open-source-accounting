# Misstatement measurement: reconcile current-year effects and accumulated balances

## Source-based principle
SAB 108 addresses errors that accumulate or reverse across periods. The staff describes the rollover approach's focus on current-period income effects and the iron-curtain approach's focus on the ending balance-sheet error. It calls for considering both approaches and relevant qualitative factors rather than relying exclusively on one. [sec-sab108]

## Prepare an error bridge
Use signed amounts and a defined convention. Record opening accumulated error, current-year additions, reversals, corrections already booked, and ending error. Reconcile the bridge to the affected accounts and financial-statement presentations. The same analysis may need separate views for interim and annual reporting.

## Original example
Assume an unsupported expense accrual increased by 12,000 in each of three years and has not reversed. The ending liability overstatement is 36,000; the current-year expense overstatement from the new accrual is 12,000. Removing all 36,000 through current-year expense would also affect the 24,000 originating earlier. These are arithmetic observations, not a determination of the proper correction method.

## Distinct judgments
Measuring an error is not the same as concluding that it is material. Separately assess each affected prior period, the current period, the proposed correction's effect, and applicable presentation/disclosure requirements. Candidate current GAAP reference: Topic 250, to be examined directly before a correction recommendation. [fasb-asc]

## Agent deliverable
Return the error bridge, both measurement perspectives, affected periods and lines, qualitative questions, proposed correction scenarios, and evidence gaps. Never net unrelated errors merely to reduce a percentage. Explain any proposed aggregation and retain the component amounts.

## Stop conditions
Do not use this article to conclude whether an amendment, revision, restatement, nonreliance notice, or other securities filing is required. Those decisions require the applicable accounting and legal requirements, current materiality analysis, and qualified review. Historical transition relief in SAB 108 is not a general current correction option.
