# Loss contingencies: build the evidence before choosing recognition or presentation

**AI-assisted educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · AI editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## Establish the question and source gap

“Reserve” is an internal label, not enough to select an accounting model. Identify the underlying matter, entity, reporting framework and period. Litigation, guarantees, tax uncertainties and ordinary contractual accruals can require different research. ASC Topic 450 is a candidate research location for loss contingencies; current primary ASC text was not accessed for this revision. Do not treat a topic reference as a verified recognition rule. [fasb-asc]

Separate the recognition, amount, disclosure and current/noncurrent presentation questions. The required criteria and exceptions must be verified for the actual matter. This article organizes evidence; it does not supply a substitute probability threshold or a complete summary of ASC 450.

## Assemble the matter record

Record the alleged event, date and nature of the demand, responsible entity, reporting date, later information, counsel's actual assessment, amount/range evidence and any proposed settlement timetable. Identify what is asserted, confirmed, disputed or unknown. Preserve changes from the previous period and evidence pointing against the proposed result.

Ask what the adviser can assess and what remains uncertain. Do not convert a model confidence score into a legal likelihood assessment, treat silence as a favorable opinion, or manufacture counsel's conclusion from a document title.

## Original claim example

An invented demand seeks USD 2000000. A later email proposes discussing a USD 1200000 settlement, but the packet contains no acceptance, merits assessment or supported estimate. Those are two different observations, not an established loss range with equally likely endpoints.

Their arithmetic midpoint is USD 1600000. The difference between the demand and proposal is USD 800000. Neither calculation establishes an accrual, an expected value, a disclosure range or legal exposure. A midpoint chosen solely because two numbers are available hides the missing evidentiary basis.

A separate fictional payment proposal divides USD 1200000 into USD 300000 now and USD 900000 later. The sum reconciles, but the proposal does not prove that payments are due, funded or binding. Preserve the status and timing before researching balance-sheet presentation.

## Four decisions to keep distinct

| Question | Evidence or authority needed | Shortcut to reject |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Applicable model and supported facts at relevant dates | Demand amount automatically equals a recorded liability |
| Measurement | Supported estimation method and uncertainty | Average every number in the correspondence |
| Disclosure | Applicable requirements, materiality and actual facts | No recorded balance automatically means no disclosure question |
| Presentation | Governing classification rules and supported settlement facts | Every uncertain balance is noncurrent |

This is an original issue map. It does not assert that these are the only questions under a particular standard.
