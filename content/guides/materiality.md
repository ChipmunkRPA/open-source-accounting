# Materiality: use a threshold to begin the analysis, not end it

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## Source-based principle
SAB 99 rejects exclusive reliance on a numerical cutoff. A percentage can begin a materiality assessment, but the surrounding circumstances matter. Relevant considerations include effects on trends, a profit-versus-loss result, contractual compliance, and management incentives. The bulletin expresses staff views; it is not itself a Commission rule. [sec-sab99]

## Build the workpaper
Record the misstatement's origin, periods affected, financial-statement lines, disclosures, and whether it is an error or an estimate change. Show the relevant benchmarks and why each helps evaluate the issue. Do not allow the spreadsheet's chosen denominator to substitute for an explanation.

Prepare a separate narrative for qualitative issues. Ask who uses the information and what conclusion could change. List evidence that supports materiality as well as evidence against it. Group related errors where aggregation could matter, while preserving their individual signs and origins.

## Original scenario
A fictional entity reports income before tax of 40,000. A 30,000 omitted charge is small relative to revenue of 20 million, but large relative to reported profit. The agent should calculate both relationships and explain why the revenue percentage alone is an inadequate stopping point. The scenario does not predetermine the full materiality judgment.

## Questions for the reviewer
Was the benchmark selected because it is informative or because it makes the error look small? Could the misstatement affect a contractual threshold? Does it change the apparent direction of performance? Is there evidence of bias or intentional adjustment? Are disclosures incomplete even when the recognized amount is small?

## Output restrictions
Return a draft assessment and evidence checklist, not a final audit materiality determination. The product should not offer a “below 5% therefore immaterial” badge. Calculations may be automated; the final judgment and its documentation remain with the responsible professionals.

## Update triggers
Reopen the assessment when additional errors, revised financial results, new covenant information, or new evidence about management intent arises. Version the workpaper so a reviewer can see which facts supported the previous conclusion.
