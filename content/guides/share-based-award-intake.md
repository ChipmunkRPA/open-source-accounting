# Share-based compensation: build the award fact file

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. **Primary ASC text was not accessed.** Paragraph references are verification tasks, not evidence that current authoritative wording was reviewed. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## Research starting point
SEC staff Topic 14 discusses interaction between Topic 718 and SEC requirements, including valuation considerations and public-company context. The current ASC text remains necessary for the actual recognition, measurement, and classification decisions. Do not extrapolate a public-company staff discussion to every private-company award without checking applicability. [sec-sab14; fasb-asc]

## Award intake
Collect the plan, award agreement, board approval, employee communication, service conditions, performance/market conditions, settlement alternatives, repurchase rights, modification history, and relevant valuation dates. Record the population and reconcile it to the cap table and compensation records.

Separate the grant date claimed by management from the evidence supporting that date. Ask whether the recipient understands the terms, whether approvals are complete, and whether any unresolved conditions affect the analysis. These are research questions, not a substitute for verifying the current criteria.

## Original scenario
A startup offers units with service vesting and a liquidity-event condition. A spreadsheet divides the nominal award value by four years. The agent should not accept that as the completed expense model. It should identify the exact conditions, evaluate the required accounting timeline using authorized guidance, and request evidence of management's estimates and approvals.

## Valuation and classification workpapers
Maintain separate schedules for instrument terms, valuation inputs, classification analysis, recognition/vesting assumptions, forfeitures, modifications, and financial-statement presentation. For options, a common-stock value alone is not an option valuation. Review the chosen method, inputs, and consistency, with a suitably qualified specialist when needed. The SEC staff recognizes more than one reasonable valuation approach in appropriate circumstances. [sec-sab14]

## Common mistakes
Do not use the preferred financing price automatically as the common-stock or award fair value. Do not treat employee income-tax consequences as the issuer's compensation expense. Do not presume every liquidity condition, cash settlement option, or modification has the same result.

## Output
Prepare a grant-by-grant unresolved-issue list, valuation evidence map, and reviewer-ready memo skeleton. No automatic “equity classified,” “no expense until IPO,” or “four-year straight line” conclusion is permitted from incomplete facts.
