# Lessee measurement: reconcile inputs before calculating

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. **Primary ASC text was not accessed.** Paragraph references are verification tasks, not evidence that current authoritative wording was reviewed. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## Scope
This worksheet starts after a potential lease has been identified. The code package's basic fixed-payment calculator is a mathematical aid, not a complete implementation of Topic 842. Current primary guidance is required for lease term, included payments, discount rate, classification, and subsequent accounting. [fasb-asc]

## Required inputs
Record commencement, payment dates, amounts, payment timing, options, incentives, initial direct costs, prepaid or accrued rent, rate methodology, and modification history. Confirm whether the rate is an effective annual rate or another convention; do not silently divide an effective annual rate by twelve.

Prepare a payment timeline before calculating present value. Identify any variable payments and the reason for including or excluding each item under the verified guidance. Have the reviewer approve the lease term and rate independently of the calculation.

## Original mathematical illustration
Assume three year-end payments of 10,000 and a confirmed annual rate of 6%. The payment present value is 10,000/1.06 + 10,000/1.06^2 + 10,000/1.06^3, approximately 26,730.12. This number is a result of the stated mathematical assumptions, not a conclusion about the required recorded lease liability or right-of-use asset for an actual agreement.

## Reconciliation and sensitivity
Reconcile the payment list to the contract and the cash forecast. Explain every difference between the computed present value and any proposed asset amount. Check that the liability rolls forward as opening balance plus interest less payment and ends near zero under the stated model. Test alternative confirmed rate or term scenarios without disguising an estimate as a mandated input.

## Matters outside the basic calculator
The initial version does not resolve classification, lease/nonlease separation, impairment, all modification rules, foreign currency, tax accounting, residual guarantees, or every variable-payment arrangement. Those omissions must appear next to the output, not only in a developer file.

## Review output
Return inputs, formula conventions, cash-flow schedule, rounding convention, exclusions, and unanswered accounting questions. Keep draft journal entries outside any automated posting path. A balanced entry alone does not establish the accounting treatment.
