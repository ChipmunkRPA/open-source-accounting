# ICFR deficiencies: evaluate potential exposure and remediation evidence

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## Source-based principle
AS 2201 requires attention to deficiency severity individually and in combination. The evaluation considers whether a misstatement could escape timely prevention or detection and its potential magnitude. An actual material misstatement is not a prerequisite to a material weakness. A compensating control must be sufficiently precise to mitigate the exposure. [pcaob-as2201, .62–.70]

## Define the deficiency
Describe what should have happened, what happened instead, the period of exposure, affected accounts and assertions, and how the issue was discovered. Distinguish a missing control, a poorly designed control, a failure in operation, and incomplete evidence about an otherwise described control.

## Original scenario
A revenue-review control was not performed for three months. A later review finds no error in a small sample. That observation is relevant but does not, by itself, resolve the potential exposure, the completeness of the tested population, or whether another sufficiently precise control operated during the gap.

## Evaluation matrix
Record the full population exposed, transaction complexity, fraud susceptibility, interactions with other deficiencies, compensating controls, and supporting evidence. Show both the likelihood-related reasoning and potential magnitude. Avoid simply labeling the issue from the dollar value of the one detected exception.

## Remediation is its own workstream
A rewritten procedure and training attendance show actions taken. They do not alone demonstrate that the revised control operated effectively for the required period. Specify the redesigned activity, owner, implementation date, evidence produced, and procedures needed to evaluate operation. These fields are this library's workpaper design, not a substitute for engagement-specific audit judgment.

## Deliverable and boundary
The agent produces a provisional issue summary, exposure map, evidence-request list, and remediation tracker. It does not issue an ICFR opinion or make a final deficiency classification from a description. The reviewer must verify the applicable AS 2201 version and any reporting obligations for the engagement.
