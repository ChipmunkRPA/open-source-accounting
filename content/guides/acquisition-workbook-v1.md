# Acquisitions: reconcile the closing packet without turning every payment into purchase price

**AI-assisted educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · AI editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## Scope and authority gap

Identify the entity, perspective, reporting framework, period, what transferred and which accounting model needs research. ASC 805 and related consolidation guidance are starting points only; current primary text was not accessed. A transaction labeled acquisition does not itself establish its scope, accounting acquirer, acquisition date or measurement model. [fasb-asc]

Obtain complete agreements, schedules, closing statements, side letters, financing, employment arrangements, ownership records and valuations. A short agreement summary cannot establish the absence of separate transactions or conditions.

## Original cash-use bridge

An invented closing packet reports USD 2000000 paid to the seller, USD 300000 paid to a lender and USD 100000 paid to advisers. The total cash-use arithmetic is USD 2400000.

| Stipulated payment | Amount | What remains unresolved |
|---|---:|---|
| Seller | 2000000 | Applicable consideration definition, terms and adjustments |
| Lender | 300000 | Which obligation was settled, by whom and under what arrangement |
| Advisers | 100000 | Nature, period and applicable treatment of the services |
| Total cash uses | 2400000 | Whether population and records are complete |

The sum is not automatically consideration transferred or an asset's capitalized amount. Reconcile each payment to its purpose and relevant accounting question before selecting a classification.

## Another amount is not automatically another purchase-price component

A separate fictional proposal offers USD 150000 if a seller continues working for a specified period. Preserve the proposal, employment conditions, status and full arrangement. Do not add it to the price as an unconditional payable or decide compensation treatment from the condition alone; the governing criteria and facts require review.

If the proposal were merely added to the cash-use total, the arithmetic would be USD 2550000. That scenario combines current payments and a conditional proposed amount. Label the distinction instead of calling the total cash paid at closing.

## Residuals and dates need evidence

Suppose a draft subtracts USD 1700000 of purported net assets from USD 2000000 and labels the USD 300000 difference goodwill. The subtraction is reproducible, but the packet has not validated scope, consideration, asset/liability population or measurement. A residual chosen to balance a journal is not a completed acquisition analysis.

Record contract signing, legal closing, transfer facts, measurement dates and when later information became available separately. A later correction is not automatically an acquisition-date adjustment. Research its nature and applicable rules rather than assigning it by convenience.
