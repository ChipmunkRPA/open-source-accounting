# Related parties: connect relationships, transactions, and disclosures

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Build a relationship map and assess evidence without assuming that management labels establish arm’s-length terms.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 850 and the applicable auditing standard**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Obtain the ownership and management structure, beneficial-owner information available to the entity, conflict declarations, board minutes, intercompany balances, guarantees, unusual customers or vendors, and transaction agreements. Record the scope of the applicable definition before classifying a party. Family, affiliate, and management relationships require facts, not name matching alone.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Create separate relationship and transaction tables linked by party IDs. Track effective dates, control or influence descriptions, amounts, settlement terms, approval evidence, outstanding balances, guarantees, and the business rationale. Reconcile the transaction population to ledger accounts and investigate unmatched legal entities and duplicate vendor records.

## Analysis workstream

Research disclosure and measurement requirements for the particular arrangement, together with any other applicable standard. Keep the financial-reporting analysis separate from audit procedures for identifying and evaluating related-party relationships. Claims that terms are comparable to market arrangements require evidence; copying contractual language that says arm’s length does not establish that conclusion.

## A failure mode to test

An employee's declaration can be an input but may not identify every indirect interest or guarantee. Likewise, an ordinary-priced invoice may coexist with favorable payment terms or an undocumented side agreement. Investigate the arrangement in its entirety rather than deciding from one price comparison.

## Deliverable and review gate

Produce a dated party map, ledger reconciliation, missing confirmations, disclosure matrix, and exceptions requiring review. Protect personal data and use role-based access for sensitive relationship details. The Agent must not infer hidden personal relationships from names, social media, or unsupported speculation.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
