# Impairment: choose the model before the valuation

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Separate asset type, unit of account, triggering events, and measurement before generating an impairment conclusion.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 350 and ASC 360**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

List the assets being evaluated, carrying amounts, allocation of associated balances, reporting units or asset groups proposed by management, useful lives, held-for-sale assertions, and relevant acquisition history. Identify goodwill, finite-lived intangibles, indefinite-lived intangibles, tangible long-lived assets, and assets governed by other guidance. Different labels may lead to different testing paths.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Create an event chronology using budgets, forecast revisions, market information, lost customers, idle facilities, legal restrictions, and changes in intended use. Preserve the forecast that existed at each decision date. Reconcile the carrying amount being tested to the general ledger and separate later transactions from conditions existing at the measurement date.

## Analysis workstream

Build a model-selection worksheet with candidate authority, applicability, testing sequence, unit of account, and required measurement concept. Then identify which estimates need valuation support. Do not combine an undiscounted recoverability exercise with a fair-value calculation merely because both use cash-flow forecasts. Document the purpose of each cash-flow series, internal consistency of assumptions, and sensitivity to significant inputs. Verify specialized exceptions and sequencing in primary text.

## A failure mode to test

A lower fair value is not, by itself, enough to run every impairment model identically. A group of assets with continuing use may require different work from a business held for sale. A management forecast prepared to obtain financing also needs evaluation before it is repurposed as an accounting estimate.

## Deliverable and review gate

Deliver a model map, evidence chronology, carrying-amount tie-out, forecast version register, sensitivities, and conditional conclusions. The reviewer should test whether favorable assumptions are consistent across budgets, disclosures, and the impairment calculation. The Agent must not label a valuation as independently appraised or professionally reviewed.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
