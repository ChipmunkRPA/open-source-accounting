# Cash flows: reconcile before classifying

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Build a transaction-level classification file rather than infer cash flows from balance-sheet changes.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 230**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Obtain beginning and ending bank reconciliations, restricted-cash schedules, the cash general ledger, debt and lease rollforwards, acquisition records, and foreign-currency movements. Record the reporting entity, consolidation scope, currency, period, and which accounts are inside the cash reconciliation. A bank transfer between two included accounts should not be counted twice as an external receipt and payment in the working data.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Construct a bridge with columns for opening balance, cash movements, noncash movements, translation or remeasurement effects, scope changes, and closing balance. Link each movement to a source document. Investigate differences before classifying cash as operating, investing, or financing. A balanced statement is a mathematical check, not evidence that its classifications are correct.

## Analysis workstream

For each unusual cash movement, identify the legal transaction, its economic purpose, the payer and recipient, whether amounts settle multiple components, and the candidate guidance. Research acquisition costs, debt fees, interest, taxes, restricted cash, supplier finance, leases, and securities using the applicable detailed rules. Do not apply one universal rule from a company's account naming convention. For indirect-method preparation, separate reconciliation adjustments from actual cash classifications and document eliminations.

## A failure mode to test

A $200,000 increase in equipment does not necessarily mean a $200,000 investing outflow: financed purchases, acquisitions, transfers, and disposals can affect the same account. An increase in accounts payable may include equipment vendors rather than operating expenses. Ask for the movement schedule, not only a comparative trial balance.

## Deliverable and review gate

Produce a reconciliation with unresolved amounts separately identified, a classification decision log, citations requiring primary-text verification, and disclosure questions. The reviewer should be able to trace every significant line to transactions and reproduce the tie-out. The Agent must stop short of declaring compliance when the opening balance, acquisition scope, or noncash population is missing.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
