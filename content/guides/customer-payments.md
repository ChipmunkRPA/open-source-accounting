# Payments to customers: route the arrangement before choosing an expense account

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. **Primary ASC text was not accessed.** Paragraph references are verification tasks, not evidence that current authoritative wording was reviewed. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## Research boundary
Payments described as marketing, rebates, implementation support, credits, or incentives can require different analysis depending on who receives them and what the payer obtains. Candidate references include ASC 606-10-32-25 through 32-27. Verify the actual current text and all related guidance before deciding whether a payment affects revenue, an asset, or expense. [fasb-asc]

FASB announced changes addressing share-based consideration payable to customers in May 2025. The announcement identifies interaction between Topics 606 and 718; it is not sufficient evidence of which amendments or effective dates apply to a specific reporting period. Route equity-linked incentives to an additional version and scope review. [fasb-customer-equity]

## Intake questions
Identify every party, the customer relationship, the amount and form of consideration, any promise made before the cash payment, the related sales, and the period of the arrangement. Ask what distinct benefit the company receives and how management estimated its value. Obtain both the sales contract and the separate service or marketing agreement.

## Original scenario
A fictional software vendor agrees to pay a customer 25,000 for a campaign. Management provides a slide stating that the campaign has strategic value, but no deliverables or valuation support. The slide is not enough to select an expense treatment. The agent should request the statement of work, proof of campaign delivery, market comparables, and connection to customer purchases.

## Evidence schedule
Use columns for payment recipient, customer status, promised service, independent benefit, valuation evidence, recognition timing, and related revenue contract. Include a separate field for whether the payment is cash, credit, or equity. Do not merge those arrangements into one generic rule.

## Output and review
Produce alternative treatments with the facts needed to support each, not a bookkeeping answer selected from the invoice description. Reconcile the commercial payment schedule to the proposed accounting timeline. Flag uncertain measurement or incomplete source access for the preparer and reviewer. This is not an authorization to post an entry.
