# Going concern: distinguish a dated funding model from the required assessment

**AI-assisted educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · AI editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## Scope and source status

Start with the actual entity, reporting framework, financial-statement date, issuance or availability date, audit-report date and proposed assessment horizon. Management's accounting analysis and the auditor's work are separate responsibilities. Do not apply one generic twelve-month window to every framework or engagement.

## Build a dated funding packet

Reconcile starting cash and restrictions, operating receipts, payment obligations, debt maturities, covenant calculations and possible financing. Distinguish available committed funds, conditional agreements, negotiations and management intentions. State what must happen before money can be used, including timing, conditions and third-party actions.

Preserve baseline and downside assumptions instead of selecting the scenario with a positive final number. An expected financing receipt must not silently become unrestricted cash at the beginning of the forecast.

## Original timing comparison

Assume USD 800000 opening unrestricted cash and six monthly payment groups of USD 150000. Planned payments total USD 900000. A proposed USD 500000 financing is uncommitted and assumed to arrive only after the sixth month's payments.

Without financing, the cumulative funding position after those payments is −USD 100000. Including the hypothetical later receipt gives USD 400000. The later receipt does not fund a payment that had to occur earlier under the stated sequence. A negative position represents unmet funding in this simplified model, not evidence that an unapproved bank overdraft exists.

Compare a second scenario in which the same USD 500000 becomes available before the sixth month's payments. Its final arithmetic can still be USD 400000, while its timing differs. The equal final values do not establish equal ability to meet obligations on time. Neither scenario predicts survival or decides a going-concern conclusion.

## Trace assumptions to evidence

For each major flow, record source, unit, timing, range and the event that could invalidate it. Test whether receipts and payments are consistently dated and whether restricted cash was excluded from the assumed usable pool. Preserve obligations that management hopes to defer unless a supported alternative is explicitly modeled.

A term sheet is not the same record as a funded facility. A written support statement is not by itself proof of the provider's capacity or that funds will arrive when needed. Those are questions for evidence and the applicable assessment criteria, not reasons for the model to assert certainty.

## Requested workpaper and review

Request the cash bridge, intraperiod timing, financing-status table, assumption sensitivities, evidence gaps and separate accounting/audit research questions. If daily payment dates are unknown, report the earliest identified monthly gap and the need for detailed timing; do not invent a specific default date.

Research how the applicable guidance treats management plans and what evaluation period and disclosures are required. Qualified review must assess implementation feasibility, effects, uncertainty and relevant reporting duties. A spreadsheet that ties does not establish compliance or solvency.
