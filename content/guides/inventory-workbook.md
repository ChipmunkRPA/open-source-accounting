# Inventory: connect quantities, costing, and recoverability

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Build a reviewable inventory workpaper that distinguishes existence, ownership, cost, and valuation questions.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 330**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Obtain inventory by item and location, count instructions, count results, ownership terms, goods in transit, consignment arrangements, production records, cost layers, overhead allocations, aging, and subsequent selling information. Identify whether the inventory is manufactured, purchased, work in process, or held for a customer. Record the costing method and the scope of any specialized industry guidance.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Tie opening quantities plus receipts and production less usage, shipments, disposals, and other movements to closing quantities. Investigate negative quantities and duplicate locations. Separately reconcile physical quantities to the costing system and then the general ledger. Do not treat inventory held by a third party as nonexistent or assume possession establishes ownership.

## Analysis workstream

Map each cost component to its source and ask which amounts belong in inventory under the applicable accounting model. Investigate unusual freight, idle-capacity costs, variances, abnormal waste, and intercompany margins. For valuation, assemble expected selling conditions, completion costs, disposition expenses, shelf life, demand, and relevant subsequent sales. Verify the method-specific measurement requirements in current guidance; do not impose the same write-down formula on every cost method.

## A failure mode to test

A slow-moving report can identify items for analysis but cannot alone establish a reserve. A seasonal item, a discontinued component, and a legally restricted item may have the same age and different economics. Conversely, recent purchases can still be impaired by damaged condition or obsolete specifications.

## Deliverable and review gate

Produce linked quantity, ownership, costing, and valuation schedules. Mark estimates and missing third-party confirmations separately. The reviewer should reconcile reserve movements and examine whether management offset unrelated gains and losses. The Agent should identify gaps and calculate explicitly assumed scenarios, not invent sales forecasts or substitute for inventory observation.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
