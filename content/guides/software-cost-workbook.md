# Software costs: organize the project facts before choosing a model

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Separate internal-use software, software for sale, and hosted-service arrangements before analyzing cost treatment.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 350-40, ASC 985-20, and applicable scope guidance**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Obtain project purpose, intended users, hosting and licensing arrangements, customer access rights, project phases, approvals, development milestones, time records, vendor invoices, abandoned features, and enhancement descriptions. Establish the reporting period and whether recently issued amendments are adopted. The label cloud or AI does not independently determine the accounting model.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Map costs to activities and dates rather than relying solely on employee job titles or purchase orders. Reconcile the project ledger to payroll allocations and vendor invoices. Identify unsupported capitalization, allocation changes, maintenance, training, conversion work, and work on unsuccessful alternatives as research items.

## Analysis workstream

Identify the relevant accounting scope and then research recognition thresholds, treatment of different cost types, commencement and cessation of capitalization, amortization, impairment, and disclosures. Keep a versioned authority map because software guidance and effective dates can change. Do not apply an old stage-gate checklist without verifying that it governs the entity's adoption status and arrangement.

## A failure mode to test

An engineer's work may contribute to several projects and activities. A single percentage applied to all engineering payroll can conceal material differences. A hosted-service implementation may also require analysis of contractual rights and the services received; an invoice labeled implementation is not enough to select a treatment.

## Deliverable and review gate

Deliver an arrangement map, cost/activity matrix, unsupported allocations, chronology, candidate accounting policies, and amortization assumptions. The reviewer should reconcile policy with approved project evidence. The Agent must not turn a project manager's retrospective estimate into verified time records.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
