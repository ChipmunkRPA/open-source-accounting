# Subsequent events: build a chronology, not a keyword label

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Organize evidence about post-period events while keeping recognition, disclosure, and audit responsibilities distinct.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 855 and the applicable auditing standard**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Collect the reporting date, issuance or availability date, audit-report date, and the date each event was discovered. Obtain legal updates, board minutes, financing agreements, settlements, customer failures, acquisitions, major casualty information, and relevant subsequent financial results. Distinguish when a condition existed from when someone learned of it.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Prepare one row per event showing the condition at the reporting date, later evidence, event date, discovery date, financial effect, records reviewed, and owner. Record uncertainty explicitly. Preserve the underlying document rather than relying solely on a generated summary or a forwarded email.

## Analysis workstream

Research whether the event supplies evidence about conditions at the reporting date or concerns a new condition, then assess the resulting recognition and disclosure questions under the applicable guidance. Separately identify the auditor's evaluation responsibilities and relevant reporting dates. Financial-statement reissuance and later-discovered facts need their own analysis; do not assume the original cutoff automatically controls every later circumstance.

## A failure mode to test

A customer bankruptcy after year-end may raise questions about conditions that already existed, while a later acquisition may raise different questions. These examples are issue prompts, not conclusions for every fact pattern. The chronology and available evidence must support the analysis.

## Deliverable and review gate

Deliver an event register, candidate accounting treatment, factual uncertainties, proposed disclosure text only where justified, and a final update checklist. The reviewer should check completeness of the search period and whether contradictory evidence was omitted. The Agent must not mark the subsequent-event review complete when the evaluation endpoint remains unknown.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
