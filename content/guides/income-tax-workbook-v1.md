# Income-tax workpaper: state the jurisdiction, assumptions and unverified law before calculating

**AI-assisted educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · AI editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## Scope and source gap

Identify the entity, jurisdiction, reporting framework, period and levy. ASC 740 is a research starting point, not an inspected scope conclusion. Current accounting requirements, enacted law, rates, elections and transition rules were not reviewed for this original exercise. No real tax position or filing instruction is supplied. [fasb-asc]

A tax department label does not decide whether a levy belongs in income-tax accounting. Keep payroll, sales, property and other obligations distinct until the applicable scope is established.

## Original stipulated-rate example

Assume fictional pretax book income of USD 100000 and, solely for mathematics, an additive adjustment of USD 20000 and a subtractive adjustment of USD 10000. The candidate base is 100000 + 20000 − 10000 = USD 110000.

Stipulate a 20% rate: 110000 × 20% = USD 22000. A separate 25% scenario produces USD 27500, a USD 5500 increase. Neither rate is described as enacted or recommended, and neither calculation establishes taxable income, current tax expense or an amount to pay.

If cash paid were separately stipulated as USD 12000, the USD 10000 difference from the 20% calculation would be a reconciliation question. It would not automatically prove a payable or an error. Identify periods, settlements, balances and other items before drawing a conclusion.

## Separate basis differences from realization judgments

A second fictional schedule shows a USD 30000 book/tax basis difference. Multiplying it by the stipulated 20% gives USD 6000. The arithmetic alone does not establish a deferred-tax asset/liability, its recognition, expected reversal, applicable rate or realization.

Record opening/closing bases, changes, jurisdiction, character, timing and relevant evidence. Do not offset amounts across entities or jurisdictions merely to make the schedule net to a desired result. Preserve supporting and contrary evidence for every proposed judgment.

## Actual law and assumptions require their own evidence

For a real matter, obtain the relevant tax records, current law, accounting source, uncertainty analysis and complete facts through authorized routes. A cash forecast is not a substitute for the accounting analysis, and historical use of a rate does not prove it remains applicable.

Identify the exact unit of analysis and whether a proposed entry depends on legal interpretation, estimates, valuation evidence or unresolved records. Keep these dependencies visible rather than presenting a single number with a false assurance label.
