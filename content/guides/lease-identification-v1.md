# Lease identification: describe the asset and rights before producing a schedule

**AI-assisted educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · AI editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## Scope and current-source gap

Recurring payments do not by themselves establish a lease, and a service label does not settle whether lease-related research is needed. Identify the entity, framework, reporting period, complete arrangement and relevant exclusions. Topic 842 is a research starting point; primary ASC text was not accessed. [fasb-asc]

The retained leases post-implementation-review reference is publisher background, not a newly verified accounting rule, amendment or effective-date schedule. Its title cannot supply the missing criteria. [fasb-leases-pir]

## Original comparison packet

Three fictional arrangements describe different commercial facts:

| Arrangement | Record supplied | Key unresolved evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A | Use of warehouse W-17 for a stated period | Complete rights, restrictions, replacement provisions and actual arrangement |
| B | Storage service across the supplier's network | Whether any asset/capacity is specified in other documents and how service is provided |
| C | Capacity described as 40 of 100 equal storage positions | What those positions represent and what rights attach to the capacity |

In C, 40/100 = 40% of the stipulated positions. This percentage does not establish the relevant accounting threshold, an identified asset or control. Equal labels and physical descriptions must not be substituted for the actual criteria.

## Separate wording from operational evidence

For a replacement provision, record what the contract says, which alternatives are actually available, whether replacement is feasible and what information supports any asserted incentive. Do not invent an economic benefit from boilerplate or decide legal enforceability from current practice.

For use decisions, identify who decides what, during which period, with what limitations and evidence. Maintenance, operation, protective restrictions and the broader commercial right may raise different questions. This article does not decide their treatment under a standard.

## Preserve the complete arrangement

Request schedules, site/capacity plans, dispatch provisions, service levels, amendments, renewal/termination terms and other linked agreements. Record missing pages or clauses and the limits of any text extraction. Absence from a retrieved excerpt is not proof that a contractual right does not exist.

An equipment serial number can be useful factual evidence but is not a complete identification conclusion. Conversely, a network service description does not justify ignoring schedules naming particular equipment.

## Requested workpaper and stop conditions

Return an arrangement-by-arrangement fact matrix, supporting and opposing evidence, unresolved contractual questions and exact primary requirements needing authorized review. Keep identification, classification, term, measurement and recognition timing as separate decisions.

Do not launch a liability schedule merely because payment data is available. Resolve the relevant model and assumptions first, or label any mathematics as a hypothetical comparison. No legal right, lease classification or current GAAP compliance is established here.
