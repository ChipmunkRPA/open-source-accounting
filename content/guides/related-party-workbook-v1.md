# Related parties: map supported relationships separately from transaction amounts

**AI-assisted educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · AI editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## Scope and source gaps

Identify the reporting entity, framework, period and applicable relationship definition before classifying a person or entity. ASC 850 and relevant auditing requirements are research starting points. Current primary ASC text was not accessed; AS 2410 remains link-only, with current applicability unverified. This article does not establish an exhaustive related-party definition. [fasb-asc; pcaob-as2410]

Use authorized ownership, governance, management, conflict-declaration and transaction records. A shared name, social connection or rumor is not evidence of a hidden relationship. Record uncertainty rather than infer personal ties from public profiles.

## Keep two linked populations

A relationship table records who is connected, how, during which period and on what evidence. A transaction table records what happened, between which legal entities, on what terms, and with what remaining balance. Link the tables through supported party identities; do not merge vendors merely because their names are similar.

Record relevant indirect interests, guarantees, side agreements, approvals and business purpose where evidence supports them. A management declaration is an input to assess, not a guarantee that the population is complete.

## Original transaction example

A fictional entity's permitted records identify a relationship requiring research with Vendor R. One invoice shows USD 5000. A comparison vendor also charges USD 5000, but R's invoice states payment after 180 days while the comparison record states 30 days. The timing difference is 150 days.

Equal invoice amounts do not establish equivalent terms, market comparability or an arm's-length conclusion. The packet lacks service scope, credit terms, side arrangements and the evidence needed to apply the relevant requirements. Neither the relationship nor the price comparison proves misconduct.

In a separate fictional ledger, a receivable from Party P is USD 70000 and a payable to P is USD 30000. Their arithmetic difference is USD 40000. Preserve the gross balances. Arithmetic alone does not establish a legal right or accounting permission to offset, settlement intent or the appropriate disclosure.

## Research and reconcile

Reconcile supported party IDs and transaction dates to ledger populations. Identify duplicate master records and unmatched legal entities as questions to resolve. A suspicious-looking entry is not automatically a duplicate or an improper transaction.

For each supported arrangement, research the applicable disclosure, measurement and other accounting requirements. Separately identify any audit procedures needed for the actual engagement. Do not describe proposed confirmation or inspection as completed work, or assume that a contractual “arm's length” label establishes the substance.

## Deliverable and privacy boundary

Request a dated relationship map, gross transaction/balance reconciliation, factual contradictions, disclosure questions and missing evidence. Limit sensitive personal details to what is necessary for the task and the authorized audience. Use actual access controls; a statement in the workpaper does not itself configure permissions.

The reviewer must verify the definition, facts and applicable requirements before classification or reporting conclusions. No allegation, professional opinion or complete relationship search is made by this educational exercise.
