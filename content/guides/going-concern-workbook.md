# Going concern: separate management assessment from audit work

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Build a dated liquidity evidence file and distinguish the responsibilities and evaluation horizons of management and auditors.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 205-40 and the applicable auditing standard**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Record the financial-statement date, expected issuance or availability date, management's assessment period, audit-report date, reporting framework, and audit regime. Obtain forecasts, cash restrictions, debt maturities, covenant calculations, financing agreements, supplier terms, board plans, and support commitments. Do not collapse these dates into a single generic twelve-month window.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Create a cash forecast tied to beginning cash, operational assumptions, contractual outflows, and available financing. Present baseline and downside cases. Distinguish committed funds from negotiations, management intentions, and aspirational fundraising. Show when minimum cash or covenant thresholds are breached, rather than only the annual ending balance.

## Analysis workstream

Research the assessment criteria and required treatment of management's plans under the relevant accounting guidance. Separately map the auditor's work, documentation, and reporting questions under the applicable auditing standard. Investigate whether plans can be implemented in time and whether their effects address the identified conditions. A financing term sheet is not the same evidence as a funded, available facility.

## A failure mode to test

Two cash forecasts with the same annual net burn can imply different short-term funding risks because receipts and payments occur at different times. A forecast with a positive December balance may still show an inability to pay a June obligation. An Agent should identify that timing problem without claiming the model predicts business survival.

## Deliverable and review gate

Deliver a timeline, documented assumptions, funding-status matrix, scenario output, disclosure questions, and separate management/auditor responsibility maps. Escalate unsupported plans and date gaps. The reviewer must verify authoritative criteria and evaluate judgment; the Agent must not issue an assurance conclusion or predict solvency.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
