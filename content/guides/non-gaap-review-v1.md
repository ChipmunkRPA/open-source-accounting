# Non-GAAP measures: inspect the adjustment and the whole presentation

**AI-assisted educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · AI editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## What was checked

The SEC staff's Non-GAAP Financial Measures interpretations were inspected on October 4, 2026. The page displayed a December 13, 2022 last-update date, with individual answers bearing their own dates. Selected answers address potentially misleading adjustments, consistency, labels, prominence and tax effects. They are staff interpretations; the applicable rules and the communication's filing context still need separate verification. [sec-nongaap]

In particular, Questions 100.01–100.06 warn that reconciliation and explanation do not automatically make a measure acceptable. Question 102.10 discusses prominence, including earnings releases furnished under Form 8-K Item 2.02. Question 102.11 addresses tax effects. This review does not establish that all later rule changes or fact-specific exceptions have been checked.

## Begin with the communication, not just the spreadsheet

Capture the actual release, filing, investor deck, webpage or script and its version. Record who will see it, when, and whether it is filed, furnished or otherwise communicated. An assumption about one channel should not silently carry into another.

Identify the proposed measure's ingredients and intended meaning before classifying it. A count of customers, a ratio and an adjusted earnings measure can raise different questions. A label chosen by management does not settle the regulatory classification. Route unclear definitions to the responsible technical and legal reviewers.

## An original arithmetic check that does not approve the adjustment

Assume a fictional issuer supplies the following draft pretax bridge in USD. The GAAP operating-income starting point is stipulated solely for the exercise. No conclusion has been made that it is the appropriate comparator in a real disclosure.

| Draft bridge | Amount | Running amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Stipulated GAAP operating income | 120000 | 120000 |
| Add back quarterly hosting cost | 30000 | 150000 |
| Add back relocation cost | 18000 | 168000 |
| Remove a gain included in the starting amount | -8000 | 160000 |

The result is 160000. If the draft reconciliation instead states 168000, it has omitted the 8000 subtraction. Correct arithmetic would not resolve whether any of the three adjustments is permissible, consistently applied or clearly described.

The hosting bill occurs each quarter under the exercise facts. Ask how excluding it changes the account of the company's operations; changing its caption to “strategic infrastructure” supplies no new evidence. For relocation, obtain actual recurrence history and future plans before using a non-recurring label. For the gain, compare the treatment of related charges in this and prior periods. These are issue-spotting prompts grounded in the staff's Section 100 discussion, not approvals or universal bans. [sec-nongaap, Questions 100.01–100.04]

## Keep four independent checks visible

1. **Adjustment rationale:** Trace every amount to the actual accounting record. Identify cash/noncash character, timing, related gains, earlier treatment and whether the adjustment substitutes a different recognition basis. “Peers do this” does not supply the company's missing analysis.
2. **Definition and label:** Compare the calculation with every use of its name. A table, headline and spoken script should not use one name for different calculations. Explain meaningful changes across periods and ask whether earlier comparisons need revision.
3. **Presentation:** Inspect the complete document, including headlines, sequence, fonts, charts and narrative. A GAAP number buried in a footnote cannot be assumed to cure all prominence issues. Save the reviewed layout as well as the numbers.
4. **Reconciliation and tax:** Tie the selected comparator and each adjustment, checking signs and rounding. Determine the correct tax treatment for the actual measure. Do not invent a uniform tax rate or combine tax into each adjustment merely to make a net number tie.

These four headings are an original review aid. They are not a complete regulatory checklist. Read the cited answers in their context, then verify the governing requirements for the actual communication.

## Stop before the compliance conclusion

Return exact text locations, the reproducible arithmetic, evidence gaps and candidate revisions for review. An automated check may identify a missing comparator or inconsistent total; it cannot clear a potentially misleading measure solely because the bridge adds correctly. Do not supply an unsupported “SEC compliant” badge.
