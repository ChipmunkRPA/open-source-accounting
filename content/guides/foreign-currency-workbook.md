# Foreign currency: separate measurement, translation, and rate policy

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Create a currency map before explaining exchange differences or proposing reclassification entries.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 830**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Identify each entity, its local books currency, asserted functional currency, presentation currency, transaction currencies, and consolidation path. Obtain transaction dates, settlement dates, rate sources, historical-rate records, monetary/nonmonetary classifications proposed by management, and changes in the economic environment. Record whether a proposed functional-currency change has actually been approved.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Reconcile transaction-level gains and losses separately from translation adjustments. Establish the direction of every rate quotation and the units used in multiplication or division. Compare opening balances, activity, closing balances, settlements, and rates. A residual translation balance should not become a convenient account for every unexplained difference.

## Analysis workstream

Research the appropriate measurement basis for each balance and the conditions for using approximations such as average rates. A month's average is not automatically interchangeable with a transaction-date rate, especially when exchange rates vary materially or transactions are concentrated. Treat long-term intercompany balances, hedges, acquisitions, and highly inflationary environments as separate analysis branches rather than extensions of a simple payable example.

## A failure mode to test

An expense booked on the last day of a month can still represent an underlying pattern of activity that requires investigation. The accounting policy cannot be inferred from a system's default rate table. Similarly, changing an ERP rate type is a configuration action whose accounting effects need testing; it is not itself an accounting conclusion.

## Deliverable and review gate

Deliver a functional-currency fact file, rate-source register, rollforward, error-versus-policy analysis, and draft entries with explicit assumptions. The reviewer should trace every exchange difference to its cause. The Agent may perform unit-checked arithmetic but must not assign functional currency or post exchange entries autonomously.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
