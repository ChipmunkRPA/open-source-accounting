# Consolidation: connect control rights to evidence

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Prepare a rights-and-economics record before selecting a consolidation conclusion.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 810**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Obtain ownership records, governing documents, investor agreements, management contracts, financing arrangements, guarantees, options, related-party relationships, and amendments. Identify the purpose and activities of each legal entity. Document direct and indirect interests rather than relying only on a capitalization table.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Map decision rights over significant activities, appointment and removal rights, participating and protective rights, funding obligations, exposure to variability, and related-party arrangements. Link each right to an exact clause and identify contingencies and exercise barriers. An ownership percentage is an input to analysis, not a substitute for identifying the applicable consolidation model.

## Analysis workstream

Research scope exceptions and whether voting-interest or variable-interest-entity analysis applies. For the selected model, identify the specific criteria, evidence supporting each, conflicting facts, and reconsideration events. Track changes in rights separately from changes in economics. Verify the current requirements for related-party relationships, de facto agents, and any specialized entity arrangements before reaching a conclusion.

## A failure mode to test

A minority investor may hold substantial decision rights, while a majority holder may face restrictions that need investigation. Conversely, a contractual label stating that someone is an independent manager does not establish the accounting significance of the actual powers retained by other parties.

## Deliverable and review gate

Produce a legal-entity map, clause-to-criterion matrix, evidence gaps, alternative-model discussion, and reassessment triggers. The reviewer should examine the entire arrangement, including guarantees outside the main agreement. The Agent should stop when material governing documents are absent and should never substitute generated summaries for legal interpretation of disputed rights.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
