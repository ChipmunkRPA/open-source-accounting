# Acquisitions: map the transaction before allocating price

**AI-assisted editorial draft · v0.6.0 · 2026-09-27 · No professional review recorded.**

Organize acquisition facts, scope decisions, measurement questions, and disclosures without turning a purchase agreement into an automatic journal entry.

## Scope and authority map

Candidate research starting point: **ASC 805 and related consolidation guidance**. This is not a complete applicability determination. Confirm the entity, reporting period, adoption status, and current primary text before relying on a rule. The platform has not reproduced the Codification or approved this article as Agent evidence.

## Intake and missing facts

Obtain the complete purchase agreement, schedules, closing statement, side letters, financing agreements, ownership chart, service arrangements, valuation reports, and board approvals. Identify what was acquired and what activities and resources transferred. Determine which business-versus-asset and acquirer-identification questions require authoritative research.

## Reconciliation and evidence controls

Construct a sources-and-uses bridge distinguishing consideration, debt settlement, transaction expenses, escrow, working-capital adjustments, contingent amounts, employee-related payments, and preexisting relationships. Reconcile cash transfers to contractual obligations. A payment at closing does not automatically become consideration transferred.

## Analysis workstream

Build a decision file for accounting scope, acquisition date, acquirer, identifiable assets and liabilities, noncontrolling interests, contingent consideration, and separate transactions. Record which fair-value estimates are provisional and why. Tie any measurement-period adjustments to information about the acquisition date, rather than automatically classifying every later correction as acquisition accounting. Verify all current recognition, measurement, and disclosure requirements before preparing conclusions.

## A failure mode to test

A seller's earnout conditioned on continuing employment deserves separate analysis from an unconditional contingent price payment. The same cash total can conceal different economics. Likewise, a closing date and an accounting acquisition date should not be presumed identical without assessing the relevant facts.

## Deliverable and review gate

Deliver a transaction map, issue register, payment reconciliation, valuation requests, draft entry alternatives, and a disclosure checklist. The reviewer should examine omitted schedules and related agreements before approving scope. The Agent must not allocate an unsupported residual to goodwill merely to make entries balance.

Record the outcome as **supported**, **conditional**, or **unresolved**, with the relevant passage or evidence gap for each material claim. A source citation without source access is a reference, not verification. Human approval must identify the exact article or workpaper revision reviewed.
