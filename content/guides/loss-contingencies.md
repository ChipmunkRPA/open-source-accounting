# Loss contingencies: recognition, disclosure, and classification are separate questions

## Establish the applicable model
“Reserve” is not an accounting model. Identify whether the issue is litigation, an ordinary accrual, a guarantee, an income-tax uncertainty, another tax, or another obligation. Topic 450 is a candidate for loss contingencies; income-tax uncertainties may require a different Topic. Verify scope before applying a generic probability-and-estimation worksheet. [fasb-asc]

## Core conceptual analysis
A typical loss-contingency inquiry considers whether a loss relates to conditions existing at the reporting date, whether an unfavorable outcome is probable, and whether the amount can be reasonably estimated. Accrual and disclosure are distinct questions. This is an original conceptual summary, not a representation that the current authoritative text was reviewed. Candidate references include ASC 450-20-25, 450-20-30, and 450-20-50. [fasb-asc]

## File structure
For each matter, record the obligation, responsible entity, alleged event date, reporting date, information available before issuance, legal assessment, amount or range, estimation rationale, and relevant disclosures. Track changes from the prior reporting period rather than copying last quarter's conclusion.

Do not manufacture numerical probability thresholds or use a model's confidence score as the likelihood assessment. If legal counsel cannot estimate a loss, ask what is and is not estimable and record that response accurately; do not replace it with a midpoint chosen by the agent.

## Original scenario
A claim seeks 2 million, but the supporting correspondence gives no assessment of the merits or likely settlement. The demanded amount is a fact, not automatically the accrual. The output should ask for the relevant probability and estimation evidence, identify potential disclosure questions, and keep the recognition conclusion open.

## Current versus noncurrent
Classify the recognized balance through the applicable balance-sheet guidance and supported settlement expectations. Do not conclude that every “FAS 5 reserve” is noncurrent because the amount is uncertain. Conversely, an amount's recognition does not establish that it will be settled within the next year. Record the operating cycle, payment timetable, and any special guidance as separate verification tasks. [fasb-asc]

## Reviewer deliverable
Return an issue register, changes log, proposed accounting alternatives, disclosure questions, and missing evidence. Consider materiality using both numerical and contextual factors rather than a universal percentage cutoff. [sec-sab99]
