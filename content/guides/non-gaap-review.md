# Non-GAAP measures: review the adjustment, label, reconciliation, and presentation

> **AI-assisted editorial draft — not professionally reviewed.** Original educational content, not authoritative guidance. Confirm the framework, reporting period, elections, and source versions before use. All company names and transactions in examples are fictional.

## Source-based principle
The SEC staff's non-GAAP interpretations warn that an adjustment can be misleading even if not specifically prohibited. They discuss recurring cash operating expenses, misleading labels, and circumstances where additional explanation does not cure a misleading presentation. The per-question revision dates and underlying rules should be reviewed for the actual filing. [sec-nongaap, section 100]

## Start with the actual proposed disclosure
Obtain the complete release, filing section, investor presentation, or other communication—not just the reconciliation spreadsheet. Identify whether the measure is a performance measure, liquidity measure, operating statistic, or another type of information, and verify the applicable requirements rather than assuming every metric is treated alike.

## Review matrix
For each adjustment, document the underlying GAAP line, amount, business rationale, frequency, cash/noncash nature, consistency with prior periods, and potential to alter the measurement basis. Compare the measure's label to its actual calculation. Review presentation and prominence in the entire document, not only in a footnote.

## Original scenario
A company proposes “adjusted operating income” excluding a cost incurred every quarter. The agent should ask why the cost is excluded, how it relates to normal operations, whether the adjustment is consistent, and how the measure is presented. It should not infer acceptability from another issuer's use of a similar adjustment.

## Reconciliation controls
Recalculate the bridge to the most directly comparable GAAP measure as determined in the reviewed analysis. Track tax effects and rounding consistently where applicable. Compare definitions across the release, deck, tables, and management discussion so the same label does not mask different calculations.

## Deliverable
Produce an issue list anchored to the proposed text, a reconciliation check, candidate revisions, and legal/technical questions. Suggested language should remain a draft. No green compliance badge should be based solely on having a reconciliation or including a disclaimer.
