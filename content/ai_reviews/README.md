# AI editorial review records

These public-safe records describe checks actually performed on exact versions
of original CC BY educational articles. They are separate from a professional
accounting or legal opinion and do not grant source permissions or Agent access.

Each record identifies the original input and revised output by SHA-256, the
actual model/settings when known, the completed review date, sources and exact
locators inspected, corrections, arithmetic checks and unresolved gaps. A source
listed as unavailable or link-only was not verified as full primary text.
Publisher restrictions and reporting-period applicability remain visible.

The index binds a record to one current article version, its retained original
input and the associated reference-metadata snapshot. A changed article,
reference record or receipt requires an explicit new review record; a previous
review cannot silently follow revised content. Missing records mean no AI review
is recorded. Malformed or mismatched records are not accepted as a review.

Outcomes distinguish bounded editorial checks passed, changes required,
unavailable primary text and unresolved rights scope. A passing editorial check
may retain clearly stated research or applicability gaps; it does not turn those
gaps into verified requirements. Failed arithmetic or blocking findings cannot
be labelled a pass. Checks are not an independently adjudicated benchmark.

The original articles, original review descriptions and original metadata retain
the [content license](../LICENSE.md). Third-party publications retain their own
rights. No credentials, private user documents, internal prompts or proprietary
execution code belong in these records.
