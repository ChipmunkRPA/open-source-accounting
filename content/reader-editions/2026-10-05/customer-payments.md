# Customer payments: identify the arrangement before selecting an expense account

## Scope and source status

Identify who receives the cash, credit or other consideration and how that party relates to the revenue arrangement. Obtain the sales contract and any separate service, marketing or incentive agreement. Topic 606 and share-based consideration references are research leads; current ASC and applicable amendments were not reviewed. [fasb-asc; fasb-customer-equity]

A marketing, rebate or strategic-partnership label does not resolve the accounting. This guide does not state a current rule for revenue reduction, asset recognition, expense or share-based awards.

## Original payment packet

An invented software vendor agrees to pay a customer USD 25000 for a campaign. Management supplies a strategy slide but no statement of work, evidence of delivery or supported valuation. The proposed payment schedule is USD 10000 initially and USD 15000 after a stated milestone.

The schedule totals USD 25000. The milestone portion is 60% of the total. Those calculations do not establish a distinct service, the service's value, satisfaction of the milestone, the date of recognition or the correct financial-statement classification.

A separate USD 5000 customer credit would bring the gross amount of the two stipulated concessions/payments to USD 30000. Keep its source, purpose and terms separate; arithmetic aggregation is not evidence that the two arrangements belong in one accounting unit or the same period.

## Evidence questions

For each arrangement, record the recipient, relationship, form of consideration, promise date, linked sales, delivery terms, conditions and actual evidence. Ask what the payer receives and how its proposed value was supported. Do not equate a broad claim of strategic benefit with a demonstrated deliverable or market price.

Track whether a promised amount has been paid, remains conditional or is disputed. A bank transfer supports a payment event; it does not prove that the underlying service was received or that the ledger caption is correct.

## Keep share-based arrangements in a separate research branch

An equity-linked incentive can introduce instrument, award-condition, measurement and amendment questions that a cash-payment spreadsheet does not answer. Identify the actual instrument, recipient, vesting or settlement conditions and reporting period. Do not use a headline financing price as an award value or assume a newer announcement is already applicable.
