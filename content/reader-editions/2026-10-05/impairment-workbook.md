# Impairment research: identify the model before comparing carrying amounts and forecasts

## Scope and source gap

Record the entity, reporting framework, period, asset or proposed grouping, carrying amounts, relevant events and intended use. ASC 350, ASC 360 and other applicable topics are research starting points; current primary text was not accessed. AS 2501 remains a link-only audit reference, not a substitute for management's accounting model. [fasb-asc; pcaob-as2501]

Different asset categories and arrangements can raise different scope, timing, grouping and measurement questions. This guide does not supply current testing criteria or decide which model applies.

## Original measurement-label example

An invented workpaper records a carrying amount of USD 900000. It contains two separate analyses: a USD 1000000 undiscounted cash-flow total and a USD 780000 value labeled by management as fair value. Neither estimate has been validated.

The numerical comparisons are +USD 100000 for the undiscounted series relative to carrying amount and −USD 120000 for the management-labeled value. Those differences answer different mathematical questions. They must not be substituted into one universal impairment formula or treated as sufficient evidence for a USD 120000 charge.

The model, unit being tested, source of the forecasts, valuation basis, required sequence and applicable criteria remain unresolved. An apparently favorable number under one concept does not validate another concept or eliminate the need for source research.

## Trace the population and versions

Reconcile the proposed carrying amount to actual asset records and identify included or excluded balances. Record the purpose and date of each forecast, the preparer, assumptions and later changes. A financing forecast can be relevant evidence but is not automatically an accounting measurement.

For every change in assumptions, show what changed and why. Preserve contrary market, operating or legal information rather than selecting the latest optimistic forecast without explanation. A source date and a measurement date are different fields.

## Separate scope, event and measurement questions

Build a map of candidate requirements, entity/period fit, proposed grouping, event chronology and estimates requiring support. Explain which missing fact could change the path. Do not treat a management label such as held for sale as an established accounting classification.

A hypothetical sensitivity that reduces a USD 200000 annual cash-flow assumption by 15% produces USD 170000, a USD 30000 reduction. The calculation shows dependence on an input. It does not establish a supportable probability, a fair value or a required impairment amount.

## Requested output and review

Request the population bridge, authority gaps, event chronology, forecast versions, alternative assumptions and conditional analysis. Keep the accounting model separate from audit procedures and independent specialist work.

The reviewer must inspect actual sources and evidence before a real recognition, measurement or disclosure conclusion. No independent appraisal, professional opinion or posting authorization is created by the exercise.
