## State the supported helper contract accurately

The inspected mathematical helper supports fixed monthly payments in arrears with a supplied nominal annual rate divided by twelve. It does not choose the rate, accept advance payments or replace analysis of term, identification, classification or right-of-use asset adjustments.

Do not enter three annual payments as three monthly payments merely because the form asks for a number of periods. An effective annual rate and nominal annual rate are different conventions; any conversion must be explicit and appropriate to the question.

## Original monthly exercise

Stipulate USD 2000 paid monthly in arrears for three months, with a 12% nominal annual rate. The monthly rate is 1%. Present value is 2000/1.01 + 2000/(1.01²) + 2000/(1.01³), approximately USD 5881.97.

This is twice the unrounded present value of an otherwise identical USD 1000-payment example. Twice the rounded 2940.99 would display 5881.98, one cent different. Recalculate from the original inputs rather than multiplying a rounded displayed total and assuming exact agreement.

## Unresolved matters remain adjacent to the schedule

Show actual payment timing, rate convention, precision and rounding, plus every omitted component. Variable amounts, modifications, foreign currency, incentives, initial costs, impairment, tax and presentation require separate analysis outside this simple illustration.

A first-payment date shown in the schedule is a label supplied by the user; it does not establish commencement or determine the legal date of a payment obligation. Retain contradictory records and incomplete extraction warnings.
