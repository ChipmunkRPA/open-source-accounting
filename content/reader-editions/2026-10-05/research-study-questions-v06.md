# Expanded study questions: checked evidence and model-selection reasoning

**Reader edition 2026-10-05.1** · Wording changes are limited to labels and notices. Earlier check statements refer to the original article revision. No new professional review or source verification is claimed.

**Educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · Editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

All 28 answers below were individually checked and revised as original research guidance. They are requested work products and bounded examples, not guarantees of automated validation, human sign-off or accounting compliance. Current primary ASC text was not accessed; these are not verified statements of its recognition, measurement or disclosure requirements. No question is promoted to evaluation-gold status.

## qa-031 — What is the first research step for cash flows?

Define the cash population, entity, currency and period. Gather bank reconciliations, restrictions, ledger detail and noncash/financing records. In an original transfer example, USD 100 moving between two included bank accounts is one internal transfer; the combined balance changes by zero. This does not classify every real transfer or establish ASC 230 compliance.

## qa-032 — What should the Agent deliver for the cash flows workbook?

Request a candidate reconciliation with unresolved differences, transaction locators, classification questions and an explicit record of checks actually performed. A tied subtotal is not a validated complete population. Missing opening balances, acquisition scope or noncash information must remain visible; no current primary ASC criterion was verified here.

## qa-033 — What is the first research step for receivable losses?

Identify the actual receivable/instrument and relevant measurement model before borrowing a historical loss rate. Gather balances, terms, disputes, collections, write-offs, recoveries and enhancements. Separate candidate credit-loss questions from billing corrections or pricing issues; the categories require evidence and applicable-authority review.

## qa-034 — What should the Agent deliver for the credit losses workbook?

Request the reconciled candidate population, segmentation rationale, rate/forecast assumptions, rollforward and sensitivity branches. Call a component validated only when the supporting procedure was actually performed and recorded. A stipulated rate calculation is not independent risk estimation or proof of GAAP compliance.

## qa-035 — What is the first research step for inventory?

Identify inventory items, locations, ownership evidence, count records, costing records, aging and later selling information. Distinguish manufactured, purchased, work-in-process and third-party-held populations without deciding ownership from location alone. The relevant costing and valuation guidance remains a research task.

## qa-036 — What should the Agent deliver for the inventory workbook?

Request linked quantity, ownership, cost and valuation questions, with differences and missing confirmations separately shown. If a hypothetical count has 100 units and records show 108, the difference is 8 units; its cause and value are not established by subtraction. Drafting assistance does not replace actual inventory observation or professional conclusions.

## qa-037 — What is the first research step for impairment?

List the assets, carrying amounts, acquisition history, management’s proposed grouping and relevant operating facts. Distinguish goodwill, finite- or indefinite-lived intangibles and other assets as research categories. Do not apply one convenient impairment calculation before verifying the applicable model and unit of account.

## qa-038 — What should the Agent deliver for the impairment workbook?

Request a model-selection question map, chronology, carrying-amount reconciliation, forecast versions and sensitivity assumptions. Compare inconsistent assumptions across budgets and disclosures. A scenario calculation is not an independent appraisal, verified fair value or approved impairment conclusion.

## qa-039 — What is the first research step for fair value?

Identify the specific instrument, rights, unit, purpose and measurement date before using a price or valuation model. Preserve observed, adjusted and unobservable inputs with their provenance. A current quote for a different security does not automatically measure this instrument, and not every internal estimate is an ASC 820 measurement.

## qa-040 — What should the Agent deliver for the fair value workbook?

Request the proposed method, input lineage, unmatched quotes, alternative assumptions and specialist questions. In a hypothetical 100-unit position, prices 9 and 10 produce 900 and 1000, a 100 difference; neither price is validated by multiplication. No independent specialist valuation or current primary ASC review is supplied.

## qa-041 — What is the first research step for acquisitions?

Obtain the full permitted transaction packet, including agreement, schedules, side letters, closing evidence, financing and ownership records. Identify what transferred and when. Business-versus-asset and acquirer-identification questions require applicable primary criteria and the complete facts, not the transaction’s label.

## qa-042 — What should the Agent deliver for the business combinations workbook?

Request a transaction map, consideration reconciliation, missing-document list, valuation questions and conditional entry alternatives. Do not allocate an unexplained residual to goodwill solely to balance a journal. The completeness of the acquired population and the accounting model must be established separately.

## qa-043 — What is the first research step for consolidation?

Map legal entities, ownership, contractual rights, decision arrangements, financing, guarantees and amendments. Separate supported direct and indirect interests from assumptions. A capitalization percentage alone is not a complete consolidation analysis; the governing model and arrangement must be researched.

## qa-044 — What should the Agent deliver for the consolidation workbook?

Request the entity/right map, clause-to-criterion questions, alternatives and reassessment triggers. Preserve missing governing documents and disputed rights. Generated summaries are not a legal determination or proof that an entity should be consolidated.

## qa-045 — What is the first research step for income taxes?

Identify each jurisdiction, entity, levy, relevant period and accounting model requiring research. Gather tax bases, provision inputs, returns, differences, carryforwards, uncertainty records and actual law changes. A department name does not decide whether a levy is an income tax, and no enacted rate is supplied by this set.

## qa-046 — What should the Agent deliver for the income taxes workbook?

Request a candidate provision reconciliation, evidence register, actual law questions and a place for real reviewer decisions. Do not generate reviewer sign-offs. A formula can be checked under stipulated rates, but current law, scope, realization or uncertain-position judgments need authorized primary research and qualified review.

## qa-047 — What is the first research step for foreign currency?

Distinguish books, asserted functional, presentation and transaction currencies; identify dates, rate sources and proposed monetary/nonmonetary classifications. Record management’s asserted currency decision and evidence. Internal approval is not by itself proof that the accounting criteria are met.

## qa-048 — What should the Agent deliver for the foreign currency workbook?

Request a currency fact file, source-dated rate register, bridge and conditional entries. For an original USD-per-EUR rate example, EUR 100 at USD 1.10 per EUR is USD 110; reversing the units would answer another question. This does not assign functional currency or authorize a posting.

## qa-049 — What is the first research step for going concern?

Record statement, issuance/availability and audit-report dates, entity/framework and the assessment horizon to verify. Gather unrestricted cash, dated obligations, forecasts, financing conditions and support evidence. Do not collapse management and auditor evaluation periods into a generic twelve-month window.

## qa-050 — What should the Agent deliver for the going concern workbook?

Request timed cash scenarios, funding-status evidence, assumptions, missing facts and separate accounting/audit questions. A positive final cash projection may conceal an earlier gap. Illustrative arithmetic is not a solvency prediction, assurance opinion or completed going-concern assessment.

## qa-051 — What is the first research step for subsequent events?

Record the condition’s asserted onset, actual event, discovery, reporting and review dates separately. Obtain relevant subsequent records with provenance and conflicting evidence. A later document can describe an earlier condition, but the description must be evaluated rather than automatically accepted.

## qa-052 — What should the Agent deliver for the subsequent events workbook?

Request the event chronology, recognition/disclosure questions, factual uncertainties and the actual evaluation endpoint. Do not mark the required review period complete while its endpoint or coverage is unknown. Reissuance and later-discovered facts need their own applicable-authority analysis.

## qa-053 — What is the first research step for related parties?

Use permitted ownership, governance, conflict and transaction records to identify supported relationships and their dates. Apply the relevant definition only after primary research. Do not infer family ties, hidden interests or misconduct from names, social-media proximity or unsupported speculation.

## qa-054 — What should the Agent deliver for the related parties workbook?

Request a dated supported-party map, gross transaction/balance reconciliation, disclosure questions and evidence gaps. Keep necessary personal details within the authorized audience. A workpaper label does not itself enforce access controls or establish a complete relationship search.

## qa-055 — What is the first research step for software costs?

Identify project purpose, users, contractual access, hosting/licensing, activities, milestones, costs and reporting period. Record which amendments and adoption choices require verification. A cloud or AI label does not determine the accounting model; later summaries do not replace contemporaneous project evidence.

## qa-056 — What should the Agent deliver for the software costs workbook?

Request an arrangement and cost/activity map, unsupported allocations, chronology and candidate policy questions. Proposed amortization assumptions remain conditional until the governing model and facts are established. A retrospective managerial estimate is not verified time-record evidence.

## qa-057 — What is the first research step for debt and equity-linked instruments?

Gather executed financing terms, amendments, side letters, conversion/redemption features, covenants, fees, guarantees and settlement alternatives. Keep issuer and holder perspectives separate. SAFE, note, preferred share and warrant labels are intake descriptions, not accounting classifications.

## qa-058 — What should the Agent deliver for the debt and equity workbook?

Request a financing map, cited selected terms, extraction-coverage gaps and candidate accounting paths. Do not promise complete term extraction from an incomplete packet. Entries, enforceability, settlement control and specialist valuation require the appropriate actual evidence and review.
