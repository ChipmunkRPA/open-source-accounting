## Intake and complete-scope questions

Select the actual draft financial statements, notes or other communication, its exact revision, entity/framework/period, filing type and source scope. Identify omitted sections and the applicable requirements actually examined. The implemented workflow requires at least one ready document, but document readiness is not a complete-checklist finding.

An incomplete source corpus cannot support an exhaustive compliance conclusion. Primary ASC text was not accessed for this playbook. The SEC Financial Reporting Manual remains non-authoritative, while the non-GAAP interpretations are staff views with question-specific dates. Treat those source categories accurately. [fasb-asc; sec-frm; sec-nongaap]

## Build requirement-to-location rows

For each issue, distinguish the governing requirement, facts making it applicable, the draft location and what the disclosure actually communicates. Use separate statuses for supported, inconsistent, missing evidence, applicability unresolved, excluded scope and not inspected. A keyword match does not establish satisfaction; absence from a retrieval snippet does not establish omission from the full filing.

For a potential non-GAAP issue, inspect the complete layout and definition, not only the reconciliation. The selected SEC staff interpretations discuss misleading adjustments and prominence, but an actual conclusion still requires applicable rules and facts. [sec-nongaap, Section 100 and Question 102.10]

## Original consistency example

An invented note states USD 250000, an earnings slide states USD 270000 and a workpaper states USD 250000 plus an unexplained USD 20000. The bridge adds arithmetically: 250000 + 20000 = 270000. Its arithmetic does not establish that the adjustment is valid, that the populations match or that either disclosure is compliant.

Record all three locations and seek the definition, period, population and support for the difference. Do not choose the repeated number by majority vote. If the draft is incomplete, state exactly which sections were not inspected.

## Requested output and review

Request the scope statement, requirement/disclosure matrix, issues ranked by consequence, proposed wording and the evidence needed to resolve each item. Candidate edits must not invent management views, materiality conclusions or commitments. Peer disclosures may suggest questions, but they do not prove acceptable wording for another entity.

For every alleged missing requirement, the reviewer checks both the primary criterion and applicable facts. For every resolved row, retain evidence of what was inspected and how it answers the issue. Revised text requires reassessment of affected findings.

The result is a draft issue analysis, not a compliance certificate or permission to file. Reading or completing this playbook does not turn on the experimental workflow, approve a source or create a human professional review.
