## Select the actual document packet

Identify permitted files, exact versions, entity/framework/period, suspected issues and known amendments or side letters. The implemented workflow requires at least one ready document in the selected workspace. A successful upload is not evidence that the packet is complete, every page is readable, or the user has licensed proprietary standards for model processing.

Record file identity, relationship to the issue and missing versions. Ask which documents form the complete agreement; do not infer that the largest or newest file supersedes all others.

## Extraction supplies candidates, not confirmed facts

The inspected preprocessing code carries explicit limits. PDF extraction does not establish visual visibility, annotations, drawn amendments, graphics, tables or completeness. DOCX extraction does not establish rendered pagination or automatic numbering. Spreadsheet extracts are stored values, not proof of recalculated formulas, units or fresh formula caches. A retrieval subset is narrower still.

These limits change the work: verify a decisive clause in the actual document context, retain unreadable or omitted areas as gaps, and do not turn absence from extracted text into a finding that a term does not exist. Sampling may identify issues but cannot by itself verify every decisive term.

## Original clause matrix

A fictional packet contains a base agreement stating a monthly fee of USD 900 and an unsigned letter proposing USD 1100. A footer on the letter says “draft,” while its filename says “final.”

| Observation | What it supports | What remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Base agreement fee text | Candidate stated term at an exact locator | Execution, continuing applicability and complete terms |
| Proposed letter fee text | A different proposed term | Whether it became operative and when |
| “Final” filename versus draft footer | A status conflict | Which authorized record establishes the operative version |

The nominal difference is USD 200 per month. That calculation does not establish a changed enforceable price, an accounting modification or a journal entry.

## Connect facts to permitted authority

For each candidate clause, state the accounting question and the exact requirement needed to analyze it. Keep “the document states” separate from “the accounting analysis suggests.” Include conflicting clauses, missing side letters and assumptions that would change the result.

## Requested output and review

Request a document inventory, clause-to-criterion map, extraction limitations, confirmed versus unconfirmed facts and a conditional analysis. Have the responsible person verify each decisive extracted passage and the full document scope needed for the conclusion. Rights for model input and downstream display/export remain separate checks.

No output from this workflow establishes legal enforceability, a complete-document review, professional approval or unrestricted rights to the uploaded files.
