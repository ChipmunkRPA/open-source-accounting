# Build an authority and reporting-period map before writing a conclusion

## Distinguish three questions

A document can be genuine, relevant to the topic and still insufficient for the decision. Ask separately: who issued it, which period/entity it covers, and whether the specific passage supports the proposed claim. A bibliography answers none of those questions by itself.

The SEC Financial Reporting Manual's landing page explicitly describes the manual as non-authoritative and says the underlying source controls in a conflict. The page inspected October 4, 2026 labels its June 29, 2026 revisions administrative and distinguishes section-specific technical updates. Do not equate the latest landing-page date with a fresh technical review of every section. [sec-frm]

Primary ASC text was not accessed for this article. AS 1105 remains a link-only audit-research reference; fresh automated retrieval was not performed after the PCAOB Terms of Use automated-gathering restriction was identified. These gaps cannot be repaired by labeling a secondary discussion “primary.” [fasb-asc; pcaob-as1105; pcaob-terms]

## Start with a scope cover sheet

Record legal entity; consolidated or separate reporting; accounting framework; issuer/filer category; engagement; period start and end; statement issuance date; transaction dates; and any claimed transition or early-adoption election. Identify which facts are confirmed and the evidence supporting them. Leave unknowns visible.

Keep the date a rule was issued, the date it became effective for this entity, the source's revision date and the date you accessed it in separate fields. Also distinguish information available at the reporting date from information learned later. Those distinctions prevent a recently published article from silently becoming the authority for an older period.

## Use one row per decisive proposition

| Claim | Nature | Evidence actually examined | Period/applicability status | Next decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-01: governing criterion | Rule or accounting requirement | Exact authorized version and paragraph, or explicitly missing | Entity and transition fit unresolved until supported | Verify before concluding |
| C-02: transaction date | Factual premise | Signed record and exact locator, if supplied | Record date may differ from operative date | Resolve factual/contractual meaning |
| C-03: alternative interpretation | Analysis | Link to supporting and contradicting facts/sources | Conditional on named assumptions | Compare consequences |
| C-04: peer wording | Company example | Actual issuer passage and context, if examined | Explains that issuer's disclosure only | Do not promote into a universal requirement |

This is an original research design, not a regulator-prescribed procedure. “Not accessed,” “partially examined” and “examined but applicability unresolved” are meaningfully different statuses.

## Original date exercise

A fictional preparer is analyzing a year ended December 31, 2024. The packet contains a teaching article updated February 2026 and a release with an assumed July 2025 effective date. These are invented dates; no actual standard is specified. The packet also lacks transition provisions and the entity's election record.

Neither the 2026 article date nor the assumed 2025 effective date proves what governed the 2024 statements. A defensible draft states the missing applicability evidence and postpones the rule-based conclusion. The useful next step is to obtain the relevant release, transition language, entity classification and documented elections through an authorized route. Selecting the newest document is not an applicability analysis.

## What a usable handoff contains

Summarize the decision, confirmed facts, decisive source passages, unverified references, alternative interpretations and the precise evidence that could change the conclusion. Report a source gap before presenting any confident answer dependent on it. An original educational article can help structure the work but cannot supply missing authoritative text or grant permission to process a publisher's material.

The resulting authority map is a research aid. It is not human professional approval, a legal opinion or evidence that the product has reviewed every listed source.
