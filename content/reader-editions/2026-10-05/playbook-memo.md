## Purpose and implementation snapshot

This is an original working method for the `memo` workflow. In the code snapshot examined October 4, 2026, it is **implemented beta and enabled by default**. Default-on does not mean unrestricted execution or professional validation. This is a source-code observation, not evidence that a particular hosted deployment or live model run passed acceptance.

## Required intake

Specify the question, intended reader, entity/framework/period, confirmed facts, relevant documents and the exact research result or draft being used. Identify the decision expected from the reader. Do not invent management's preferred treatment, a legal conclusion or a commitment to take action.

## Build the memo from its evidence table

Start with facts and authority, then analyze the alternatives. Distinguish source-backed criteria, factual assertions, original arithmetic and accounting inferences. Keep any missing primary passage prominent. An SEC Financial Reporting Manual reference remains non-authoritative context; the underlying rule or applicable standard is still needed. No current ASC primary text was accessed for this playbook. [sec-frm; fasb-asc]

Use a compact structure: decision question, conditional summary, facts, applicable requirements, analysis, meaningful alternatives, conclusion, calculations and open items. This is an original organizational choice, not a mandatory professional memo format.

## Original calculation paragraph

Suppose a fictional draft assumes 30 identical service units at USD 400 each. The arithmetic total is USD 12000. An unsigned amendment proposes USD 450 per unit, which would produce USD 13500 on the same assumed population, a USD 1500 difference.

A draft may show both branches and their assumptions. It must not treat the unsigned proposal as an operative price or convert either total into recognized revenue without the applicable facts and requirements. The executive summary should preserve that dependency, not bury it in a footnote.

## Editing and export are separate from new AI execution

The inspected product supports manual creation/editing and reading/export of existing memos without a subscription check at those endpoints, while retaining workspace and output-rights checks. This does not mean every user can read every memo or export every source passage. New AI drafting or a follow-up Agent run uses the ordinary execution gates.

Keep the exact saved revision, title/body and references when requesting review. An edit creates a new revision; it does not transfer a previous review declaration to new wording. Existing reviewer declarations record what their authors said about a particular revision and do not themselves verify professional qualifications.

## Requested output and review

The useful result is an editable draft with supported citations, explicit alternatives, checked arithmetic and an open-items list. Proposed entries remain drafts, not postings. A reviewer should inspect contradictory evidence and the limits on each quotation or export, then record actual scope and findings for the exact revision reviewed.

Do not promise that completion of a drafting task produces an independent opinion, a legally sufficient memo or an automatically approved accounting policy. If a required source or decisive fact is missing, the conclusion stays conditional.
