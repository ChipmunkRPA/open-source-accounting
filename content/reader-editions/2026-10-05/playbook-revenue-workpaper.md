## Intake and model questions

Select the operative agreement and amendments, relevant period, actual promises, delivery evidence, prices/credits, variable terms and proposed allocation inputs. Identify which accounting criteria need authorized current research. Primary ASC text was not accessed for this playbook. [fasb-asc]

Keep billing, collections, allocation and recognition in separate parts of the workpaper. A contract-economic total can reconcile while the reporting model or timing remains unresolved.

## Use the helper only for its stated mathematics

The inspected sample allocation helper performs proportional allocation using supplied weights, with a disclosed whole-cent policy and exact accepted weight values. It does not decide whether promises are distinct, a price is an appropriate standalone price, an allocation exception applies or an amount should be recognized.

For an original exercise, stipulate a USD 54000 total and weights of USD 45000 and USD 15000. Total weights are USD 60000; the 75:25 proportions produce USD 40500 and USD 13500. The sum is USD 54000. The premises remain fictional and do not represent a real accounting review.

## Evidence and exceptions

For each supplied input, record source, date, unit, status and evidence that could invalidate it. Do not pass a missing value as zero merely to make the helper run. A zero value accepted mathematically does not settle the applicable accounting question.

Where the arrangement includes variable amounts, financing, changes, other promises or a possible exception, state the model boundary and obtain appropriate authority before selecting treatment. A fallback flat percentage is not a resolution.
