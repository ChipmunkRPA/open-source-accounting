## Original cost-allocation packet

An invented payroll pool is USD 100000. For an exercise with uniform hourly cost, the records describe 100 hours: 60 associated with development, 25 with maintenance and 15 with training. The hours sum to 100.

Under that explicitly stipulated allocation basis, the amounts are USD 60000, USD 25000 and USD 15000. The labels and arithmetic do not establish eligibility for capitalization or expense, nor do they verify the timesheet.

A blanket 80% allocation to the development label would produce USD 80000, USD 20000 more than the stated 60-hour branch. That difference identifies the dependence on the input basis; it is not automatically a misstatement or a USD 20000 correcting entry.

## Trace activities, dates and actual records

Link payroll and invoices to specific activities, milestones, evidence and the project population. Record the difference between contemporaneous records and later estimates. A retrospective project-manager percentage is an assertion, not verified time documentation.

Preserve abandoned alternatives, changes in purpose, training, maintenance and other activities as items requiring analysis. Do not omit them from the intake merely because they might produce an inconvenient outcome.

## Keep the model and schedule questions open

Research recognition thresholds, eligible costs, timing, amortization, impairment and disclosure under the actual applicable requirements. Internal approval of a project is relevant factual evidence but does not itself settle those criteria.

For a separate purely mathematical proposal, USD 60000 divided evenly over 36 assumed months is approximately USD 1666.67 per month before addressing cumulative cent handling. The multiplication of that rounded display by 36 is USD 60000.12, revealing a twelve-cent rounding difference. Neither the USD 60000 base nor the 36-month period has been established as an accounting input.

## Requested output and review boundary

Request the arrangement map, activity/cost reconciliation, evidence dates, alternative assumptions, missing records and exact source questions. Show any schedule as conditional and reconcile its rounding explicitly.

A balanced project ledger does not demonstrate correct capitalization, useful life or impairment. The responsible reviewers must establish current authority and actual facts before approving a policy or posting. No such professional conclusion or operation is supplied here.
