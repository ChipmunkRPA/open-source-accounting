# Source onboarding: verify the material and each intended operation

**Reader edition 2026-10-05.1** · Wording changes are limited to labels and notices. Earlier check statements refer to the original article revision. No new professional review or source verification is claimed.

**Educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · Editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

## Identity and scope

Record publisher, title, edition/version, exact official link, authority type, language, target sections, source date, applicable period and planned audience. Distinguish the work from the website hosting it. A government page may contain third-party material; a standard's title may identify the topic without providing the operative text.

Record the proposed acquisition method before using it. The ability to open a page, the right to make a copy, the right to send text to a model and the right to redistribute an output are separate questions.

## Two publisher-policy observations

The SEC Webmaster FAQ inspected October 4, 2026 states its reuse position for government-created SEC content and EDGAR public filing content and notes exceptions. Do not infer from this that every attachment was authored by the U.S. government, or that protected material embedded in a publication has been relicensed. Examine the actual material and relevant policy before acquisition. [sec-reuse]

The PCAOB Terms of Use inspected the same day contains an Authorized Use restriction on automated information gathering. Its separate Public Materials provisions describe conditional reuse. For this review, no automated PCAOB standard retrieval proceeded after that restriction was identified. A downstream reuse allowance does not by itself resolve an acquisition-method restriction. [pcaob-terms]

These are source-specific observations, not a blanket finding that all standards or all public materials are unusable.

## Original intake example

A fictional researcher proposes copying a 200-page private handbook into a searchable Agent source because it can be downloaded without paying. The intake record has a title and URL, but no reviewed basis for copying, model use or hosted redistribution. The proposed operations therefore remain unresolved. Rewriting the entire handbook through a model is not a substitute for resolving those rights. The Copyright Office explains that fair use is case-specific, with no fixed quantity formula that guarantees permission. [fair-use]

The researcher can instead prepare an original issue outline and identify the specific primary sections needed, without representing an inaccessible passage as examined. If a licensed route later becomes available, record its actual terms and scope; do not invent a license from the existence of a user account.

## Version, attribution and release checks

Retain exact artifact identity, measured hash where an artifact was lawfully acquired, acquisition date, permission record and required attribution. Do not invent a hash for a link-only reference. Check embedded third-party content, modification conditions, rate limits, allowed users, provider/region, retention and removal requirements relevant to the intended operation.

Recheck changed source text or terms before carrying prior permissions forward. Keep each operational decision in the product's authorized process. This article neither imports a source nor changes its rights, review status or Agent eligibility.
