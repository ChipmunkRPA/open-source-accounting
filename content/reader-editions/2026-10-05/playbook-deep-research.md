## Intake that changes the answer

Write one decision question, identify the entity/framework/reporting period and list the facts already established. Separate accounting questions from contractual or legal questions requiring different authority. Record the documents and source passages available, the permitted uses and the precise gaps. Do not treat an unavailable standard as available because its homepage is in the catalog.

## Work in propositions, not just search terms

Divide the issue into claims needed to reach a conclusion. For each, record whether it is a factual premise, a governing criterion or an inference. Search the permitted corpus for supporting and contrary evidence. Keep the exact source/version/locator and enough context to assess exceptions. The current pipeline is bounded; a search over admitted sources is not proof that all relevant literature was searched.

The SEC Financial Reporting Manual describes itself as non-authoritative and points to underlying authoritative material in a conflict. Use it to locate questions and sources, not to fill a missing primary-text requirement. Primary ASC text was not accessed for this playbook. [sec-frm; fasb-asc]

## Original issue tree

A fictional customer agreement has an initial service date of July 1 and a proposed amendment dated June 28. The packet contains the draft amendment but no evidence of execution or the operative effective date. The reporting period ends June 30.

A useful issue tree separates: what was agreed by period-end; which accounting requirements apply to those facts; and what the later record can establish. The dates alone do not answer enforceability, recognition or modification treatment. Returning an explanation of missing evidence is more useful than selecting the earliest date and writing a confident answer.

## Requested output, to verify

Ask for a concise conditional conclusion, fact matrix, claim-to-source map, analysis of alternatives and a prioritized missing-evidence list. Each decisive sentence should either point to inspected support or clearly state its assumption. Include the strongest contrary evidence and explain what would change the conclusion.

Label proposed follow-up research as proposed. Do not imply that a model searched inaccessible subscriptions, verified every citation or contacted a publisher. A statement such as “all relevant standards considered” is unsupported when the corpus or primary access is incomplete.

## Handoff and stopping condition

Stop the dependent conclusion when a decisive passage is unavailable, applicability is unresolved or facts conflict. Continue the independent parts: organize the known facts, calculate a stated example and list precise evidence requests. The responsible reviewer checks actual passages and the exact answer revision; a citation list or another model response is not professional approval.
