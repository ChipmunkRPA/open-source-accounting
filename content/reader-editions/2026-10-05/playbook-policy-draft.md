## Intake

Identify the business model, entity/framework, reporting period, current approved policy, documented elections, transactions in scope and actual decision owner. Record unknown decisions rather than allowing the model to choose an election or management position by default.

Primary ASC text was not accessed for this playbook. An existing internal policy or peer policy does not substitute for the actual governing requirements; it is a record to evaluate against them. [fasb-asc]

## Separate the layers

Keep accounting principle, entity-specific election, implementation procedure, control responsibility and disclosure wording in distinct sections. A procedure can describe how staff gather evidence without deciding what the accounting must be. A policy label does not override an unresolved contractual fact.

Retain prior policy versions and describe each substantive change, its proposed reason and the approval still needed. A generated date or signature line is not evidence of adoption.

## Original drafting example

A fictional draft says “all setup fees are recognized immediately when invoiced.” The packet contains no analyzed contract population, current authoritative support or approved election. The correct drafting action is to identify those missing prerequisites, not to polish the sentence into an authoritative-sounding rule.

A separate procedure could instead propose collecting signed terms, delivery facts and the relevant approved accounting analysis before processing a fee. That is an original process suggestion, not a claim that this wording alone satisfies the applicable standard.

## Requested output

Request a scoped draft, decision register, alternative wording, examples, unresolved criteria and an approval field to be completed only by an actual authorized person. Do not supply fabricated reviewer sign-offs or silently replace the existing approved policy.

The responsible management and technical reviewers decide whether and when to adopt a policy and how to apply it. No posting, adoption, publisher permission or professional certification follows from a generated draft.
