## Use and scope

These original questions teach research habits and explicitly stipulated calculations. All 30 answers received an exact-revision editorial check, including fresh permitted SEC/GAO source checks where stated. This is not an adjudicated accounting benchmark or permission to ingest private standards. Primary ASC text and the linked PCAOB standard/policy bodies were not freshly reviewed; affected answers are research prompts or original reasoning, not verified statements of those requirements.

Question IDs are retained for traceability. This article’s revised wording does not automatically change the separate question bank or confer human approval or evaluation-gold status.

### qa-001: Does an ASC citation prove that an Agent examined the standard?
No. A citation can identify a research location without proving access. Check the actual passage, version, access record and its support for the claim. Current primary ASC text was not accessed for this set.

Related library item: authority-and-period. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-002: Is the SEC Financial Reporting Manual authoritative?
The SEC landing page inspected October 4, 2026 describes it as non-authoritative and says underlying authoritative material governs a conflict. Its latest page date does not establish that every technical section is current.

Related library item: authority-and-period. References: sec-frm.

### qa-003: Can a peer disclosure alone establish the correct accounting?
No. It records what that issuer disclosed in its own circumstances. Identify relevant differences and verify the governing criteria; common practice and similar wording are not regulatory approval.

Related library item: authority-and-period. References: sec-frm.

### qa-004: Does a nonrefundable implementation-fee label prove immediate revenue?
The billing label alone does not establish a recognition conclusion. Research what is promised, whether and when anything transfers, the applicable criteria and the complete agreement. This answer is an issue-spotting instruction, not a verified ASC 606 rule.

Related library item: saas-implementation. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-005: Which facts help frame implementation-service research?
Collect the scope of work, deliverables, customer use, possible alternative providers, integration activities, acceptance terms and platform-access timing. Explain what each fact might change. The list is original research design, not an authoritative decision checklist.

Related library item: saas-implementation. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-006: What allocation follows from a stipulated 80:20 ratio and USD 90000 total?
Pure arithmetic gives USD 72000 and USD 18000, which sum to USD 90000. The stipulated ratio is not evidence of valid standalone selling prices, distinct obligations or the absence of an accounting allocation exception.

Related library item: case-ssp-allocation. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-007: Does an invoice date alone establish revenue timing?
No recognition conclusion follows from that date alone. It is billing evidence. The agreement, performance facts, framework and actual applicable recognition requirements remain separate research tasks.

Related library item: revenue-research-map. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-008: Can a convenient percentage be applied to every contract?
Not merely because it makes a schedule tie. Identify the actual model, support its inputs and research relevant exceptions. An arithmetic ratio without that work is only a stated assumption.

Related library item: revenue-research-map. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-009: How should a payment to a customer be investigated?
Identify the recipient and arrangement, contractual purpose, goods or services said to be received, pricing evidence and the relevant reporting period. Research applicable customer-consideration guidance rather than classifying the payment from a marketing label. Primary ASC text remains unaccessed.

Related library item: customer-payments. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-010: What additional research is needed when customer consideration is share-based?
Examine how the customer arrangement and share-based terms interact, including scope, vesting conditions, applicable amendments and transition/election facts. The FASB customer-consideration announcement remains a research lead; the source list links only to the FASB homepage. Its direct page body and the complete ASU/current ASC text were not verified in this review; no effective-date or treatment conclusion is supplied.

Related library item: customer-payments. References: fasb-customer-equity.

### qa-011: Does calling a contract a service rule out lease-related research?
No conclusion follows solely from the label. Establish the actual asset/use arrangement, relevant rights and other terms, then compare those facts with authorized current requirements. This answer does not decide whether a lease exists.

Related library item: lease-identification. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-012: Does a three-year payment schedule establish lease classification?
No. A schedule calculates stated inputs. Classification, contractual term, commencement, components and the rate need their own factual and authoritative support; none is established by the number of rows.

Related library item: lease-measurement. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-013: What is the present value of three year-end USD 10000 payments at 6%?
Using annual compounding and the stipulated timing, 10000/1.06 + 10000/(1.06^2) + 10000/(1.06^3) rounds to USD 26730.12. Keep full precision until the final cent rounding. The exercise does not establish a lease discount rate or recognized liability.

Related library item: case-lease-present-value. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-014: Why can displayed rows fail to sum exactly to a rounded total?
Individual rows and the total may be rounded at different stages. Define the method, retain unrounded values and reconcile the difference. Do not use rounding as an explanation until the actual discrepancy has been reproduced.

Related library item: case-lease-present-value. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-015: Is an award’s headline value enough to book compensation?
No accounting amount is established by the label alone. Gather the actual award, grant/service chronology, conditions, settlement alternatives, modifications and valuation evidence. SEC Topic 14 Section C offers limited staff context on model fit and expertise; it does not verify the fictional award or replace unaccessed ASC text.

Related library item: share-based-award-intake. References: fasb-asc, sec-sab14.

### qa-016: Can an amendment be assumed to leave the expense pattern unchanged?
No such conclusion is supplied. Preserve the original and amended terms, execution/effective-date evidence, recipient facts and valuation information, then research current applicable criteria. A text change alone does not establish its legal or accounting effect.

Related library item: share-based-award-intake. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-017: Can an internal reserve label establish current/noncurrent classification?
No. Separate recognition, measurement, disclosure and presentation questions. Identify the actual obligation, dates and settlement evidence, then verify the applicable classification criteria. This set did not access the governing ASC paragraphs.

Related library item: loss-contingencies. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-018: Should every tax uncertainty and loss matter use one generic model?
No model can be selected from the word reserve alone. Identify the levy or obligation and the scope requiring research. Topic 740 and Topic 450 are candidate research locations, not verified scope conclusions for a particular matter.

Related library item: loss-contingencies. References: fasb-asc.

### qa-019: Is 5% an automatic immateriality rule?
No. SEC SAB 99, Topic 1.M.1, rejects exclusive reliance on a numerical cutoff. The staff discussion requires attention to relevant circumstances; it does not supply a universal alternative percentage. The official August 12, 1999 bulletin was inspected October 4, 2026.

Related library item: materiality. References: sec-sab99.

### qa-020: Does a small amount always make an error immaterial?
No. The quantitative and qualitative context still matters under SAB 99’s staff discussion. A small revenue percentage may coexist with a changed profit trend or another significant effect; the actual facts are needed before a conclusion.

Related library item: materiality. References: sec-sab99.

### qa-021: Why reconcile both income effects and accumulated balance errors?
SAB 108 discusses both perspectives, including prior-year carryover and reversal effects. An ending balance corrected to zero can still leave a current-year income distortion. Keep measurement, materiality and the appropriate correction treatment separate.

Related library item: error-measurement. References: sec-sab108.

### qa-022: If an error adds USD 12000 in each of three years without reversal, what accumulates?
USD 36000 at the third year-end: USD 24000 from earlier years plus USD 12000 newly added. If all 36000 is released through current-year expense, the original +12000 expense error becomes 12000 - 36000 = -24000 under that stipulated path. Neither amount decides materiality or approves the release.

Related library item: case-accumulated-error. References: sec-sab108.

### qa-023: Can an error-free sample alone resolve a control-deficiency question?
The observation alone does not establish the complete exposed population, risks, procedure quality or appropriate classification. Assemble that evidence and verify the applicable criteria. AS 2201 remains link-only here; current severity requirements were not freshly examined.

Related library item: icfr-deficiencies. References: pcaob-as2201, pcaob-terms.

### qa-024: What is needed before treating another activity as a compensating control?
Identify what it actually did, its timing, inputs, precision, coverage, exceptions and evidence. Do not assume a description or owner’s assurance resolves the original gap. This is an original evidence request, not a verified application of current AS 2201.

Related library item: icfr-deficiencies. References: pcaob-as2201, pcaob-terms.

### qa-025: Does approval of a redesigned control prove it operated?
No. Approval, implementation, execution and later review are distinct events. Preserve their true dates and supporting records. The resulting remediation/classification judgment requires applicable criteria and qualified review; this set does not supply it.

Related library item: case-control-remediation. References: pcaob-as2201, pcaob-terms.

### qa-026: Do ten copies of one report provide ten independent observations?
No: under the stipulated exact-copy relationship there is one distinct report represented ten times. In real work, verify provenance before calling records duplicates. Assess relevant evidence qualities under authorized applicable guidance; current AS 1105 text was not freshly reviewed here.

Related library item: electronic-audit-evidence. References: pcaob-as1105, pcaob-terms.

### qa-027: How can an externally produced electronic report be prepared for review?
Record who produced it, the original source/version, how it reached management, transformations, period, population and unexplained gaps. Preserve the original where permitted. The linked PCAOB electronic-information policy was not freshly retrieved after its site access restriction was identified, so no current policy requirement is presented as verified.

Related library item: electronic-audit-evidence. References: pcaob-electronic-policy, pcaob-terms.

### qa-028: Can explanatory notes cure every misleading non-GAAP measure?
No. SEC staff Question 100.06 explains that detailed disclosure may still leave a measure materially misleading. Review the measure and applicable presentation requirements; arithmetic and explanatory text alone do not establish compliance.

Related library item: non-gaap-review. References: sec-nongaap.

### qa-029: What dates does GAO state for the 2024 Yellow Book?
The GAO page inspected October 4, 2026 states that financial audits, attestation engagements and financial-statement reviews generally use periods beginning on or after December 15, 2025; performance audits use audits beginning on or after that date. Early implementation is permitted. The general quality-management design/implementation deadline is December 15, 2025, with evaluation by December 15, 2026. A separate federal-government-audit-organization note permits design/implementation deferral no later than March 16, 2026 and evaluation no later than one year after implementation. Do not apply that special quality-management note as a blanket deferral of all engagement-effective dates.

Related library item: yellow-book-scope. References: gao-yellowbook.

### qa-030: Does paying for Agent execution license proprietary standards?
No. Ordinary subscription entitlement is one software-execution gate. Workflow enablement, source permissions, scope, budget and other controls remain separate. Reading original educational content stays free; no ASC body or publisher license is supplied by this study set.
