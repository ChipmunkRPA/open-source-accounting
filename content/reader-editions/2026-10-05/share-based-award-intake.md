# Share-based awards: reconcile the register before choosing a model

**Reader edition 2026-10-05.1** · Wording changes are limited to labels and notices. Earlier check statements refer to the original article revision. No new professional review or source verification is claimed.

**Educational draft · v1.1.0 · 2026-10-04 · Editorial checks only; no human professional review.**

Build a fact file that separates recorded award units, issued shares, offered items, chronology and valuation inputs. This original fictional issuer-side employee option-record exercise develops the starter guide's promised population reconciliation. It does not determine a grant date, classification, vesting, recognition, valuation, tax result or legal right.

## 1. Define the populations and original packet

Every identifier, quantity, date, price and record below is invented. These are teaching labels, not real documents, approvals, retained hashes or evidence of enforceable rights. The snapshot is September 30, 2026. The award register's unit is a **Plan O option unit**; the cap table's unit is an **issued common share**. Both are descriptions in the fictional records, not verified accounting or legal conclusions.

For the exercise alone, all base-register movements use the same Plan O unit definition and period, with no other movements. Applying their signs constructs a candidate administrative reconciliation; it does not establish that an entry, exercise or cancellation is operative. Real completeness, cutoff and validity remain unresolved.

| Packet label | Stipulated observation | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| O-OPEN | Opening register reports 10000 Plan O units | Opening support and full population still needed |
| ADD-A | Event E-101 enters 4000 units for batch A | Complete event/participant/terms evidence still needed |
| ADD-A-COPY | Export repeats E-101 with the same fields and lineage as ADD-A | An exact duplicate is stipulated here; retain both source rows |
| ADD-B | Distinct event E-102 enters 4000 units for batch B | Equal quantity does not make it a copy of E-101 |
| EX-01 | Administrative log removes 1500 units on September 12 | Recorded movement, not an adjudicated legal exercise |
| CAN-01 | Separate log removes 500 units on September 20; the raw export omits it | Proposed bridge item, not a forfeiture or rights conclusion |
| OFFER-01 | Separate tracker has 2000 proposed units awaiting stated steps, outside this base register | Preserve it for analysis; pending does not mean no accounting |
| CAP-01 | Share record attributes 1500 issued common shares to EX-01 | A one-share-per-removed-unit mapping is stipulated only for this exercise |

The raw export contains O-OPEN, ADD-A, ADD-A-COPY, ADD-B and EX-01. CAN-01 comes from a separate supplied record. If an event identity or its fields conflict, retain the contradiction and stop selecting a unique-event amount until it is resolved. Neither matching quantities nor a matching ID alone proves that real records are duplicates.

## 2. Reconcile by event identity

The first table records what the fictional export reports. Signed movements are additions or removals from an administrative population, not journal entries, expenses or legally vested quantities.

| Export row | Event identity | Quantity contribution | Unit |
|---|---|---:|---|
| O-OPEN | Opening snapshot | 10000 | Plan O unit |
| ADD-A | E-101 | 4000 | Plan O unit |
| ADD-A-COPY | E-101 | 4000 | Plan O unit |
| ADD-B | E-102 | 4000 | Plan O unit |
| EX-01 | EX-01 | -1500 | Plan O unit |

The raw subtotal is **10000 + 4000 + 4000 + 4000 − 1500 = 20500 units**. Preserve that observed export separately from the candidate reconciliation.

| Candidate bridge row | Change from preceding amount | Running quantity | Basis in this exercise |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Raw export subtotal | 20500 | 20500 | Five rows above |
| Remove stipulated exact E-101 copy | -4000 | 16500 | ADD-A-COPY; retain original rows |
| Include separately recorded removal | -500 | 16000 | CAN-01; its validity remains a review question |

An independent movement view gives **10000 + 4000 E-101 + 4000 E-102 − 1500 EX-01 − 500 CAN-01 = 16000**. The raw export exceeds this conditional candidate by **4500**, with two separately traceable causes: 4000 from the stipulated copy and 500 from the omitted administrative movement. This is not an approved correction to the original register or a compensation adjustment.

Keep these counterexamples visible:

- Collapsing the two legitimate 4000 additions into one because their quantities match gives **12000**, understating the stipulated candidate by **4000**. Deduplication requires supported identity and field reconciliation, not amount equality
- Appending OFFER-01 gives **18000**, which is **2000 above** the deliberately defined recorded-register population. The offer stays in the evidence file; this boundary does not decide whether or when it has an accounting effect
- If CAN-01 remains disputed, the unique-event export before that proposed movement is **16500**. Keep the **500** unresolved instead of calling either branch a legally outstanding award balance

Do not change a source row or invent a missing approval to make a subtotal tie. A termination notice, a register status and an operative cancellation may raise different evidence questions; the packet resolves none of those judgments.

## 3. Reconcile issued shares separately

The separate fictional share ledger has no other movements under the exercise assumptions:

| Share-ledger row | Recorded share movement | Running issued-common-share quantity |
|---|---:|---:|
| CAP-OPEN | 500000 | 500000 |
| CAP-01 | 1500 | 501500 |

Thus **500000 + 1500 = 501500 recorded issued common shares**. Under the supplied mapping, EX-01 removes 1500 Plan O units while CAP-01 adds 1500 issued shares. Those are different quantities in different populations. They do not cancel inside one register, and they are not two funding or award additions.

The arithmetic sum **16000 + 501500 = 517500** combines unlike labels. That arithmetic does not establish an issued-share count, fully diluted capitalization, ownership percentage or compensation measure. The one-for-one mapping was supplied solely for this example; no settlement ratio is inferred for a real award. No exercise price, cash proceeds, withholding or tax amount is calculated.

## 6. Study questions and bounded answers

These questions and their arithmetic received editorial checks, but have not been professionally adjudicated. They are embedded in this guide and remain outside the unchanged 90-record question bank.

1. **Why retain two 4000 additions but remove the third in the candidate?** The exercise stipulates distinct E-101 and E-102 events and an exact copy of E-101. Matching quantities alone would not support that decision in real records.
2. **What explains the 4500 bridge?** The stated 4000 copy and 500 omitted administrative removal. Their arithmetic does not establish the legal or accounting validity of either event.
3. **Can the 16000 candidate be added to 501500 shares?** The labels have different units and meanings. Those amounts alone do not establish an issued-share or fully diluted capitalization measure.
4. **Does the May 15 board record establish management's claimed grant date?** No determination is supplied. Preserve the complete chronology, terms and evidence questions for the applicable-authority analysis.
5. **What does the 24000 shortcut establish?** Only arithmetic on nominal and assumed inputs. It does not support valuation, recognition, period allocation, classification, taxes or a journal entry.
