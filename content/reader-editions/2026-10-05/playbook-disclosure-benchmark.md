## Intake and sampling purpose

Define the actual research question, entity/framework/period and why a peer example might help. Set a factual peer-selection rationale, document types, dates and authorized acquisition route. The technical workflow has no minimum uploaded-document requirement, but that does not mean it already possesses a complete filing corpus or has fetched any actual peer filing.

When permitted filings are actually examined, record issuer identity, accession/date, form, exact section and the version used. Record any excerpt or acquisition limits. A search-result headline, copied paragraph or old accession does not establish the most recent filing or the required context.

## Original comparison example

Invented Peer A describes one revenue measure for a twelve-month period; invented Peer B uses the same label for a nine-month period and excludes a business sold during the year. Even if both show USD 900000, equal amounts and labels do not establish comparability.

A hypothetical per-month division gives 900000/12 = 75000 and 900000/9 = 100000. Those are simple average-period amounts, not seasonality-adjusted performance, annualized forecasts or comparable GAAP measures. The difference identifies missing scope information; it does not justify rewriting the peers' disclosures into one common figure.

## Compare the context before borrowing the presentation

Record business scope, transaction facts, definitions, reporting periods, framework and relevant elections. Explain both similarities and differences. A peer can suggest a useful disclosure question or way to organize data; its wording does not prove that the treatment is required or accepted for another company.

The SEC Financial Reporting Manual calls itself non-authoritative. It may help locate staff discussion, but neither it nor a peer's filing replaces the applicable authoritative requirement. The SEC reuse FAQ does not make all issuer attachments government-authored or eliminate source-specific review. [sec-frm; sec-reuse]

## Requested output and stopping point

Request the selection rationale, exact sources inspected, comparability matrix, concise permitted examples and unresolved questions. Distinguish “not located within this sample” from “no issuer discloses this.” A small or convenience sample cannot establish prevalence across all filers.

Do not imply regulator approval from public availability, lack of a visible comment letter or repeated market practice. If no actual filings were obtained, return the research plan and missing-source statement rather than invented peer passages. No filing, disclosure or source grant is authorized by this guide.
