# IRS primary-source pilot — 4 October 2026

Ray Sang’s Annotation — AI-assisted source-catalog description; not professionally reviewed.

This bounded pilot preserves eight complete IRS Revenue Ruling PDF originals and their complete extractable text. Source identity, exact bytes/hashes, dates, pagination, authority and rights evidence are recorded in manifest.json. A source document is not a newly authored article. Primary text retains U.S. Internal Revenue Service attribution and is not relabeled as first-party copyright or restricted by new first-party noncommercial terms.

The IRS reuse policy permits copying work created or maintained by federal employees in their official duties and requests credit, while excluding third-party copyrighted material: https://www.irs.gov/about-irs/use-of-content-from-irsgov

The full texts were read for this exact-item AI source/material screening. Newly reacquired PDF hashes match those exact reviewed originals. Every document identifies authors from IRS Chief Counsel offices. Cited/quoted materials in this batch are federal statutes, regulations, IRS/congressional material or federal judicial opinions; no private treatise excerpts or publisher-added headnotes were identified. pdfimages reports no embedded raster images. This is not a human professional legal/accounting review or a blanket .gov reuse rule.

Revenue Ruling 2023-2 is excluded because it includes definitions from private legal reference works. The retained advance copy of Revenue Ruling 2007-3 is excluded because it contains an unresolved publication placeholder. Publications containing possible third-party photographs are outside this pilot.

Seven rulings have identified IRB publication presence/dates. The retained files are the actual standalone advance-PDF editions; exact reconciliation to final IRB text remains open. Revenue Ruling 2026-20 remains explicitly an advance copy. Published revenue rulings are fact-specific IRS interpretations and do not have the force of Treasury Regulations. Subsequent statutes, regulations, rulings and judicial decisions must be checked before applying an old source. No current-law claim, professional approval, live application import or Agent access grant is made here.

No IRS/Treasury endorsement, sponsorship or affiliation is implied. Only original catalog descriptions are Ray Sang’s Annotation; the source PDFs and extracted primary text are government materials. No fictional examples or Premium implementation are included.
