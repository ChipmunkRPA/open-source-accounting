This is an initial research/source-acquisition catalog for all 50 states and the District of Columbia, with representative local coverage. It is **not a complete tax library, current-rate service, legal opinion, or filing tool**.

## What was actually retained

- 154 original HTTP artifacts across 175 recorded source URLs; 21 blocked/deferred source URLs remain visible
- 52 IRS referral/index pages, counted separately from substantive state/local sources
- 102 state/local publisher artifacts: 12 whole official PDF documents (206 pages) and 90 official HTML pages/indexes
- 56 tax-agency referral links across 51 baseline jurisdictions; agency landing pages retained for 30 jurisdictions
- 560 additional observed tax/authority links, separately classified as discovery candidates rather than acquired documents
- Zero source bodies published here, zero current rates verified, zero professional approvals, and zero automatic Agent admissions

The retained originals are held outside this public repository for source-specific rights and substantive review. This public directory contains reference metadata and original coverage descriptions only. ## Jurisdiction and authority boundaries

State, county, city and special-district layers require separate evidence. A state revenue department may administer a local levy; the administrator and taxing geography are distinct fields. Source IDs back every positive local-layer observation. The geographic matrix is not an enumeration of every county, municipality or district.

The Washington B&O source is tagged gross receipts separately from income tax. Franchise, gross-receipts, commercial-activity, transaction-privilege and general-excise terminology cannot be treated as interchangeable taxes merely because they appear in the same research family. Classification and tax-base review remain source- and jurisdiction-specific.

Representative records distinguish California FTB income/franchise materials from CDTFA sales/use and district materials; New York State DTF from New York City Finance; and Washington DOR from Seattle municipal source URLs. Seattle's robots resource returned 403, so those city sources remain link-only and their bodies were not requested. CDTFA's canonical-host redirect was verified separately, after which its canonical robots policy and selected sources were retained.

Administrative tax tribunal decisions, agency rulings, agency summaries of court cases, and judicial opinions have different authority. A tribunal or case-summary index is not a retained judicial opinion. This batch includes state case/ruling discovery indexes; individual state-court decisions have not been acquired or validated in this catalog.

## Dates and historical editions

Retrieval time, HTTP modification time, an identifier year, publication edition, tax year and effective date are separate. Five California form/table tax years were checked against retained PDF page 1. Two Washington city lodging notices state July 1, 2026 as an effective date; no numerical rate is reproduced or certified as current. New York publications 900 and 750 bear 2011 and 2015 edition labels respectively, so they must not be presented as current without amendment/supersession review.

An as-of timestamp records acquisition/observation, not certification that a rule was still in force. Supersession and jurisdiction-specific professional applicability review remain open. File-path dates are not silently converted to effective dates.

## Rights and validation

Source-body copyright and third-party/codifier components are unresolved unless separately reviewed. Government hosting alone does not make all state/local material public domain. External login, paywall, robots and HTTP denials were not bypassed. Metadata-only publication does not expand source or Agent permissions.

This catalog received bounded automated integrity and AI metadata checks, including review of source identity, hash/byte counts, referral separation, local-layer evidence, federal candidate exclusions, dates, public-body exclusion and false-admission flags. These checks are not human legal/accounting approval.
