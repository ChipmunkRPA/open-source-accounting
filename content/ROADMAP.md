# Content development and review roadmap

## Release 0.5.0 — built
- 14 research guides covering revenue, leases, stock compensation, contingencies,
  materiality/error analysis, audit evidence, internal controls, and government auditing.
- 5 fictional worked cases, 6 reusable templates, and 16 Agent playbooks.
- 30 original Q&A records plus a readable study guide.
- 22 reference records with publisher, URL, verification scope, and reuse boundary.
- Public reading/search/download, hash validation, database staging, and separate review gates.

## Next editorial work
1. Independent CPA/accounting reviewer: audit every substantive claim, scope,
   authoritative locator, and hypothetical result. Record actual evidence and open gaps.
2. Correct/split items as needed, assign new immutable versions, and retain prior
   review records rather than replacing them with blanket approval.
3. Add deeper examples for contract modifications, variable consideration,
   material rights, principal/agent, lease changes, and award modifications.
4. Create approved source acquisition records for selected SEC/GAO publications.
   Do not automatically mirror publisher pages or issuer attachments.
5. Add genuinely verified primary/secondary evidence packets for the five core
   agents. Review rights and technical content separately before enabling inference.
6. Evaluate accounting outputs with a professionally adjudicated case benchmark.
   The current study questions are not a validated gold standard.
7. Expand private-company audit and IFRS coverage only with adequate licensed or
   otherwise reviewed sources. Do not infer missing rules from U.S. GAAP commentary.

## Not yet done
No human accounting review, licensed standards integration, live ingestion schedule,
complete EDGAR corpus, current ASC verification, or deployed/public GitHub release
is asserted by these checklists. See root progress.md for publication status.
