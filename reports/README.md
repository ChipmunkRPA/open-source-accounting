# Validation provenance

`bootstrap/` contains results actually rerun during issue #6 integration. Logs named `initial` include the observed pre-build failure. Other reports arrived in the v0.7 archive and are **historical evidence only**, even when their filenames lack a release label. Content reports regenerated from unchanged manifests measure inventory, not professional approval or live acquisition.

`bootstrap/reconciliation.json` identifies every archive member and its import disposition. The original ZIP hash is recorded there; ZIP and compiled assets are not committed.
